# Preface

If you're holding this book, there's a good chance you're looking for something. Maybe you're looking for answers. Maybe you're looking for motivation. Maybe you're looking for a way out of a situation that feels impossible. Or maybe you're just tired. Tired of starting over. Tired of making promises to yourself that never seem to last. Tired of feeling like you're capable of more but never quite becoming the person you know you could be.

If that's you, I understand, because I've been there.

Before we go any further, I want to tell you what this book is not. This is not a book written by a millionaire. It's not written by a celebrity, and it's not written by a guru. It's not written by someone who has life figured out. In fact, if someone tells you they have life figured out, they're probably lying, selling something, or have become complacent. Life doesn't work that way. We're all adapting. We're all learning. We're all becoming.

I'm no different. I'm an above-average medical sales representative, a husband, and a father. A man who has made mistakes, who has failed, and who has carried pain. A man who spent year after year making excuses — and a man who finally got tired of lying to himself.

This book didn't begin as the book you're about to read. Originally, I set out to write about life skills — the practical things I felt weren't being taught. Credit card debt. Insurance. Sales. Real estate. Financial responsibility. The kinds of lessons I wish more young people understood before learning them the hard way. Those topics still matter, and they always will. But something unexpected happened while I was writing. Every lesson led to a story, and every story led to another lesson. And every lesson eventually forced me to look in the mirror. The deeper I wrote, the deeper I went. What started as a book about life skills became a book about awareness. What started as a book about knowledge became a book about truth. Because the more I reflected on my own life, the more it sank in that most of my problems weren't caused by a lack of information. They were caused by a lack of honesty. I knew what I should do; I just wasn't doing it. I knew what needed to change; I wasn't changing it. And for years, I had an excuse for everything.

The truth is that excuses are comfortable. Excuses protect us. They help us avoid responsibility. They allow us to blame circumstances, people, timing, bad luck, our past, and everything else except the person staring back at us in the mirror. I know, because I did it longer than I'd like to admit.

This book is the story of what happened when I finally stopped. It's the story of childhood trauma, and of a mask that followed me into adulthood. It's the story of alcohol, anxiety, depression, failure, and self-deception. It's the story of losing a business. It's the story of sitting in a dark room, holding my daughter, believing the people I loved would somehow be better off without me. It's the story of finally understanding that the greatest obstacle in my life wasn't the people around me.

It was the person I had become.

Most importantly, it's the story of rebuilding. Not perfectly. Not quickly. Not dramatically. One decision at a time. One habit at a time. One truth at a time.

Along the way, I learned something that changed my life. Actually, I learned a lot of things, but they all seemed to lead back to one question — a question that still sits on a Post-It note on my bathroom mirror, a question I ask myself every day, a question that changed everything.

Who do I have to become?

Not: What do I want? Not: How do I get there? Not: Why is this happening to me? But: Who do I have to become? That question forced me to stop looking outward and start looking inward. It forced me to stop blaming, to stop hiding, and to stop waiting. Most importantly, it forced me to tell myself the truth.

The truth is that this book isn't really about me. It's about you. It's about the stories you're carrying, the excuses you're believing, the habits you're repeating, and the future you're building — whether you realize it or not. You may be carrying things nobody knows about. You may feel behind. You may feel stuck. You may feel exhausted. You may feel like you've wasted years, or like you've gone too far in the wrong direction.

I want you to hear something before you turn the page. Your story is not over. You are not finished. You are not disqualified. And you are capable of far more than you currently believe. I know that, because I once believed the exact opposite.

This book won't solve your problems. No book can do that. What it can do is help you ask better questions. It can help you become more aware, and it can help you tell yourself the truth. And if you're willing to do that, everything can change. Not overnight, but permanently.

So before you continue, I want to ask you the same question that changed my life — the same question that sits on my mirror, the same question that echoes throughout every chapter you're about to read.

Who do you have to become?

Answer that honestly, and this book might change your life. Avoid it, and this will simply be another book. The choice, as always, is yours.
