# Dedication

To Avery and Isla,

You are the reason I kept going.

There was a time in my life when I believed the lies — a time when I thought the world would somehow be better without me in it. I was wrong. Now I can see that God placed both of you in my life exactly when I needed you most. You gave me purpose when I felt lost. You gave me hope when I couldn't see it for myself. You gave me a reason to become the man I was meant to be.

You have watched me run. You have watched me fail. You have watched me get back up. You have watched me keep promises. You have watched me become. And the truth is that I have been watching you too.

Everything I hope to teach you can be summarized in one sentence:

Never stop becoming.

You are capable of far more than you realize. Do hard things. Tell yourself the truth. Love people deeply. Protect your integrity. And never let excuses decide the direction of your life.

If this book leaves behind any legacy at all, I hope it is this: that you know how deeply you are loved, and that you always remember that your dad kept fighting because of you.

Love,
Dad
