## §5.3 — The Weekly 4M Reflection

Fifteen minutes every Sunday evening. The single highest-leverage habit in the entire system. The men who do this for one year look back and don't recognize the man who started.

Pour something warm. Sit somewhere quiet. Take a pen. Run the four pages below in order.

### Page 1 — MIND (the destination)

Cognitive healthspan. What you're protecting.

> **This week, my Mind felt:**
> _(sharp / clear / foggy / scattered / sluggish — pick one or write your own)_
>
> **One specific moment of cognitive clarity I noticed:**
>
> **One specific moment of cognitive friction I noticed (word loss, mental fatigue, fog):**
>
> **What likely contributed?**
> _(sleep, alcohol, training, stress, food timing, supplement compliance)_
>
> **One adjustment for next week to support Mind:**

---

### Page 2 — MITIGATE (stop hurting yourself)

What you removed or reduced this week.

> **Insulting behaviors I successfully avoided this week:**
> _(alcohol nights, late-night eating, screens past 9, fluorescent lighting after dark, etc.)_
>
> **Insulting behaviors I did not avoid this week (no judgment, just data):**
>
> **The single insult I want to remove next week:**
>
> **Mitigate stack compliance (rate each: 0-3):**
> ☐ Biome NS Ultra daily __ ☐ SleepRestore nightly __ ☐ Sleep window held __
> ☐ Eating window closed at 6 PM __ ☐ Alcohol below 7 drinks __

---

### Page 3 — MUSCLE (the engine)

Strength, nutrition, fuel.

> **Training sessions this week:** __ (target: 2-3)
>
> **Protein-first breakfast hit days:** __ (target: 7)
>
> **Sunrise walk days:** __ (target: 7)
>
> **One PR or notable training moment:**
>
> **One nutritional win:**
>
> **One nutritional slip and the trigger behind it:**

---

### Page 4 — MOTIVATE (the why)

The recursive engine. The reason you do this.

> **Who am I doing this for? Name the face:**
>
> **One moment this week that reinforced my why:**
>
> **One moment this week I wanted to quit (everyone has them):**
>
> **What kept me showing up?**
>
> **One specific action my accountability person can hold me to next week:**

---

### Page 5 — The Single Adjustment

After completing the four pillar pages, write ONE sentence:

> **The single change I am committing to for next week:**

That's it. One. Not five. The men who change everything every week change nothing. The men who change one thing per week, every week for a year, are unrecognizable to themselves at the end of it.

Date the page. File it. Read it at the start of next Sunday's reflection.

This is how compounding actually works. Not dramatic resets. Honest weekly accounting + one small adjustment, fifty-two times in a row.
