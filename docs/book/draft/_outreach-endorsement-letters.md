# Endorsement / Foreword Outreach

Two assets:
1. **Master outreach letter** — adaptable template.
2. **Target shortlist** — name → angle → who to route through → ask size (foreword vs. blurb).

## Target shortlist

Ranked by a mix of audience fit, plausibility, and warm-path availability. Tier 1 = big swing with a real lever. Tier 2 = high-fit / cold but credible. Tier 3 = peer-clinician anchors (book needs at least one of these for credibility).

### Tier 1 — Big swings (foreword candidates)

**Tony Robbins**
- *Why him:* Mass audience overlap. Massive credibility on identity, purpose, and "do not lose yourself." *Life Force* (his book with Peter Diamandis) is functionally a regenerative-medicine on-ramp for his audience — your 4M framework is the operational layer for what he points at.
- *Warm path:* Your MD partner had a recent chance meeting with him. **This is the real lead — work this one first.** Get a 60-second elevator from the MD partner, plus the personal connect.
- *Angle to lead with:* "Your *Life Force* readers asked you the right question — *now what do I actually do every day?* This book is the daily field guide for the framework you put on the map."
- *Ask:* Foreword. Fallback: blurb.

**Peter Attia, MD**
- *Why him:* Owns the longevity-clinician audience. His "Medicine 3.0 / four horsemen" framing is exactly the upstream lens your Mitigate section runs on.
- *Warm path:* Cold to start. Realistic ask = blurb, not foreword.
- *Angle:* The ED-as-canary thesis + the gut-brain → cognitive trajectory framing are the parts of *Outlive* he did not have room to operationalize. Your book operationalizes them.
- *Ask:* Blurb. Stretch: foreword.

**Mark Hyman, MD**
- *Why him:* Functional medicine + cognitive (he wrote *The UltraMind Solution*). Your gut-brain-axis chapter sits directly inside the territory he has been the loudest voice on for twenty years.
- *Warm path:* IFM network — check if any of your A4M / regenerative-medicine peers have a direct line.
- *Angle:* The 4M order — putting Mind as the *destination*, not just another pillar — gives a clean structural answer to a question functional medicine has been circling.
- *Ask:* Foreword realistic. Blurb minimum.

**Andrew Huberman, PhD**
- *Why him:* Owns the male, performance, neuro-curious audience under 50 — the readers most likely to act early.
- *Warm path:* Cold. Realistic = blurb.
- *Angle:* Light, sleep, environment, and morning-sun protocol in the Mitigate section is directly his playbook applied at the lifestyle-program level.
- *Ask:* Blurb.

### Tier 2 — High-fit / cold but credible

**David Sinclair, PhD** — longevity hardware credibility. Ask: blurb. Angle: regenerative arm of the program (Chapter 10).

**Dave Asprey** — owns the biohacker / self-experimenter audience that maps perfectly to the "I used myself as the first guinea pig" thread in your preface. Ask: blurb. Angle: the self-experimentation lineage from L-arginine in high school → TRT at 40 → regenerative now.

**Ben Greenfield** — peer self-experimenter audience, podcast reach, regenerative-curious. Ask: blurb. Probably easier yes than Sinclair/Attia/Huberman.

**Gary Brecka** — Ultimate Human podcast, mass reach, regenerative + functional angle. Already operating in the lane the book lives in. Ask: blurb. Plausible foreword.

**Dr. Daniel Amen** — owns cognitive-decline / brain-health authority with mainstream credibility. Ask: blurb. Angle: the 4M order putting Mind as the destination is exactly the structural argument he has made for twenty years.

### Tier 3 — Peer-clinician anchors (need at least one)

A4M / regenerative-medicine peer leader — someone with a diplomate and a recognizable name in the specialty who can vouch for protocol rigor. The book needs at least one of these alongside any celebrity / mass-audience name; without it the back cover skews entertainment, not medicine.

Candidates to consider from your direct network:
- A4M faculty or board member you have worked with.
- A regenerative-medicine peer running a recognized clinic.
- Any MD who has published in the andropause / TRT / cognitive-decline space and knows your work.

You will know the names here better than I will. **Send me 3-5 you would actually warm-call and I will tailor the letter for each.**

## Sequencing recommendation

1. **Tony Robbins** — work the warm lead now while it is fresh. Ask MD partner to set the bridge. Do not send the letter cold to his team; have it land *through* the MD partner with a personal note attached.
2. **One Tier 3 peer-clinician** — get a hard yes here first. Having a credentialed endorsement already secured makes the Tier 1 asks easier — you are not asking for a launch, you are asking them to join a lineup.
3. **Tier 1 cold sends** — Attia, Hyman, Huberman, Amen in parallel. Realistic expectation: 1 of 4 responds, 1 of those responses is a blurb. That is a win.
4. **Tier 2 fallback** — Asprey, Greenfield, Brecka, Sinclair if the Tier 1 round goes quiet after 3 weeks.

Send no more than 4 outreach letters in any given week. If you send 12 at once and three say yes, you have a sequencing problem and a thank-you problem.
