# Appendix C — Eliminate the Insulting Behavior Checklist

Mitigate works in one direction at a time. Add a protocol AND keep doing the thing that broke you, and you get no progress — you get a wash, an expensive wash. The order is the order: stop the insults first, then add what works. Print this page. Read down each pillar. Check the boxes that apply to you. If you check three or more in any category, that's your first thirty days of work — not a new supplement.

---

## Mitigate — the gut, the brain barrier, the environment

**Gut**

- ☐ Drinking more than 7 alcoholic beverages per week (LPS leakage, dysbiosis, leaky gut)
- ☐ Eating processed foods and seed oils as a daily baseline (microbiome stripping, chronic inflammation)
- ☐ Multiple antibiotic courses without targeted recovery afterward (scorched-earth microbiome, no replant)
- ☐ Routine NSAIDs, PPIs, or chronic acid-blockers (intestinal-lining damage, impaired digestion)
- ☐ Ignoring bloating, food sensitivities, or brain fog after meals (symptom denial — the gut is talking)
- ☐ Treating yogurt and kombucha as a "gut protocol" (random probiotics ≠ strain-specific repair)

**Sleep**

- ☐ Bright overhead light and screens past sundown (suppressed melatonin, delayed sleep onset)
- ☐ Alcohol within 3 hours of bed (fragmented REM, suppressed glymphatic clearance)
- ☐ Late caffeine — anything after 2 PM (half-life is 6 hours; you don't feel it but your brain does)
- ☐ Eating inside the 3-hour pre-sleep window (insulin and reflux degrade deep sleep)
- ☐ Bedroom warmer than 67°F or lit by any visible LED (impaired core-temperature drop)
- ☐ Treating "I'll sleep when I'm dead" as a personality (you'll get there faster than you planned)

**Environment**

- ☐ No morning sunlight in the first hour after waking (broken circadian anchor, downstream sleep collapse)
- ☐ Drinking unfiltered tap water by default (chlorine, fluoride, microplastics, pharmaceutical residue)
- ☐ No air filtration at home or office (VOC and particulate load you can't smell)
- ☐ Plastic food storage and bottled-water habit (endocrine-disrupting chemicals, daily dose)
- ☐ Phone on the nightstand, Wi-Fi router in the bedroom (EMF exposure during the only window the brain repairs)
- ☐ Indoors under fluorescents or LEDs all day, no outdoor breaks (light starvation, vitamin D collapse)

**Hormones**

- ☐ Never had a full hormone panel — guessing from symptoms (you can't fix what you haven't measured)
- ☐ Chronic under-sleeping while expecting normal testosterone (sleep is when T is made)
- ☐ Carrying visceral fat and assuming it's cosmetic (aromatase converts T to estradiol — fat is endocrine)
- ☐ Stress without recovery — no breath work, no walks, no decompression (chronic cortisol, suppressed gonadal axis)
- ☐ Plastic, parabens, conventional personal-care products (xenoestrogen load)
- ☐ Accepting the 1%/year T decline after 30 as "just aging" (it's optional, not destiny)

**Substance use**

- ☐ Daily alcohol of any volume (there is no protective dose — that study was wrong)
- ☐ Nicotine of any form (vape, pouch, gum) on a daily basis (vascular and dopamine damage)
- ☐ Recreational cannabis as a sleep aid (suppresses REM, builds tolerance, ages the hippocampus)
- ☐ OTC sleep meds nightly (anticholinergic load — Benadryl is on the dementia-risk list)

## Muscle — the engine, the metabolism

**Weight**

- ☐ Grazing all day with no eating window (constant insulin, no fat oxidation)
- ☐ Skipping protein at breakfast and crashing into carbs at lunch (no 30g lean-protein-first anchor)
- ☐ Drinking your calories — juice, soda, sweetened coffee, beer (liquid sugar bypasses satiety)
- ☐ Cardio-only with no resistance training (you can run a marathon and still be sarcopenic)
- ☐ Weighing yourself daily and ignoring waist circumference (visceral fat is the metric that matters)
- ☐ Sub-1500 calorie diets that strip muscle along with fat (especially on GLP-1 without protein and lifting)

**Nutrition**

- ☐ Industrial seed oils — canola, soybean, corn, sunflower, safflower — in any "premium" food (oxidized PUFAs, mitochondrial damage)
- ☐ Eating breakfast carbs first thing — cereal, pastries, sweetened oats (blood-sugar rollercoaster, late-morning crash)
- ☐ Restaurant and takeout meals more than 3x/week (no control over oils, sodium, hidden sugar)
- ☐ "Low-fat" packaged products (sugar substitution, ultra-processed)
- ☐ Artificial sweeteners — sucralose, aspartame, acesulfame — daily (microbiome disruption, glucose dysregulation)
- ☐ Not knowing your protein target by bodyweight (under-eating protein for the goal you have)

## Mind — cognitive, peptides, the long signal

**Cognitive**

- ☐ Dismissing brain fog as "just getting older" (it's a signal, not a milestone)
- ☐ No daily learning or novel-skill input (the brain prunes what you don't use)
- ☐ Multitasking and constant context-switching all day (chronic attentional fragmentation, cortisol)
- ☐ Doom-scrolling 60+ minutes daily (dopamine baseline reset, attention erosion)
- ☐ Avoiding cardio and resistance work despite cognitive complaints (BDNF is generated by movement)
- ☐ Ignoring family history of cognitive decline (the window for prevention is now, not at diagnosis)

**Peptides**

- ☐ Buying research-grade peptides off the internet (no sourcing chain, no dose verification, no oversight)
- ☐ Stacking peptides without a coach or labs (peptides are tools, not multivitamins)
- ☐ Running peptides indefinitely with no cycling (receptor down-regulation, diminishing returns)
- ☐ Using peptides to compensate for sleep, diet, or stress you refuse to fix (you cannot out-peptide a bad protocol)

## Motivate — the foundation under the foundation

- ☐ No specific person you can name when asked "who are you doing this for?" (no accountability target = no follow-through)
- ☐ No written "why" — only a vague intention (untethered goals collapse under stress)
- ☐ No weekly review or cohort accountability (compliance decays in isolation)
- ☐ Treating the protocol as a phase, not an identity (phases end; identities don't)

---

If you checked more than five items total, you don't need a new supplement this week. You need a Mitigate week — stop the insults, hold the line, and let the system catch its breath. Then come back here, pick the next box, and close it.

Stop hurting yourself first. Then add what works.
