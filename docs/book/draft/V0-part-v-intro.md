## How to Use This Action Guide

You've reached the back of the book. If you read straight through from Chapter 1, you have spent the last few hours understanding the system. Now you need to run it.

This section is built differently from the rest of the book. The chapters were prose. The Action Guide is structured for use. You are not meant to read it once and put it on a shelf. You are meant to print it, mark it up, and refer to it daily for the first month.

Here is what's in it:

- **§5.1 — Daily Check-Ins.** The four daily behaviors that produce 80% of the compounding. If you do nothing else from this book, do these.
- **§5.2 — The Stack.** Your daily supplement dosing card. What to take, when, with what, why.
- **§5.3 — The Weekly 4M Reflection.** One page per pillar. Fifteen minutes every Sunday evening. The single highest-leverage habit in the entire system.
- **§5.4 — The Adherence Scorecard.** The tear-out page. If this were a printed book, it would be perforated so you could pull it free and tape it to your bathroom mirror. Because the book is digital for now, you have two options: print this page, or photograph it for your phone lock-screen. Pick one before you finish reading this paragraph.
- **§5.5 — The 30-Day Recommit.** When you fall off — not *if*, *when* — this is the page you come back to. It is not a punishment. It is a procedure.

This guide mirrors the daily check-ins, weekly Zoom calls, and adherence scorecards that run inside the My4MLife app. The app does the tracking for you; this guide does the same work on paper, for the men who still think better with a pen in their hand.

The first 30 days are the hardest. They are also the most leveraged. By day 30, you will have built the architecture that makes compliance the path of least resistance for the next 30 years.

Begin with the end in mind. The end is your mind. Let's protect it.
