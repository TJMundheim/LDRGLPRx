# Month 1 Meals — rule, first-meal template, 12 dinners, video plan
**Locked 2026-09-24 (TJ):** 12 dinners a month (3/week, built for leftovers at lunch); plan named **"high-protein, low-carb"**, each recipe marked *keto-compatible*; never a "keto plan" banner. Same four weeks for every cohort, forever. Cooking videos by the **Keto Cattle Baron** (TJ), phone, one take, 3–5 min, cut to 60-s shorts.

## The rule (every recipe passes all of it)
1. **Protein first.** 35–50 g protein per serving at dinner. Lunch = last night's leftovers.
2. **No seed oils, no sugar, no flour.** Fats: butter, ghee, tallow, olive oil, avocado oil, coconut oil.
3. **As few carbs as the dish allows.** Vegetables are the carbs. No grains, no potatoes, no sweeteners except a pinch of monk fruit where noted.
4. **20–30 minutes hands-on.** If it can't be cooked in the time the video claims, it's out.
5. **No weak links.** Every packaged ingredient passes the full-ingredient audit (broth, sauces, spice blends).
6. **Fasting:** 16:8 is the cohort default (first meal ~11, last bite ~7). Count Yourself Skinny "light start" (12:12 → 14:10) for anyone who has never fasted.
7. **No alcohol in recipes or on camera.** Iced tea / green tea on the table.

## The non-negotiable first meal (template, three rotations)
Break the fast, every day: **gut-restore scoop in 8–12 oz water** *with* the meal, then **30–40 g lean protein**.
- **A. Eggs + meat:** 3 eggs + 3 oz leftover dinner protein, spinach wilted in the pan, salt. (~38 g)
- **B. The bowl:** 6 oz plain Greek yogurt (full-fat or 2%) or cottage cheese + 1 scoop unflavored collagen + berries (½ cup) + walnuts. (~40 g)
- **C. The plate:** 5 oz smoked salmon or leftover chicken, ½ avocado, cucumber, olive oil, lemon. (~35 g)
Coffee after, not before. Water first.

## The 12 dinners (each serves 4 = dinner + 2 lunches; macros per serving are estimates)

### Week 1 — "Cook once, eat twice"
1. **Cattle Baron Ribeye + Skillet Greens.** Ribeye seared in tallow, rested; kale/chard finished in the pan drippings with garlic. ~48 g protein, ~6 g net carbs. 20 min. *Buy:* ribeye (ButcherBox), tallow, greens.
2. **Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs, Broccoli & Lemon.** Bone-in thighs, broccoli, olive oil, lemon, salt, 425°F 30 min. ~42 g, ~7 g. 10 min hands-on. *Buy:* thighs (ButcherBox), broccoli.
3. **Ten-Minute Salmon + Asparagus.** Skin-on salmon, butter, asparagus in the same pan, dill. ~40 g, ~5 g. 12 min. *Buy:* wild salmon (ButcherBox), asparagus.

### Week 2 — "One pan, one pot"
4. **Ground Beef & Cabbage Skillet.** 85/15 beef, shredded cabbage, onion, smoked paprika, finished with butter. ~44 g, ~8 g. 20 min.
5. **Pork Chops, Brussels & Mustard Cream.** Thick chops seared, Brussels halved in the pan, splash of cream + Dijon. ~46 g, ~9 g. 25 min.
6. **Chicken "Fajita" Bowl, no tortilla.** Chicken breast strips, peppers, onion, cumin/chili, over shredded lettuce with avocado and sour cream. ~45 g, ~10 g. 20 min.

### Week 3 — "Slow and easy"
7. **Sunday Chuck Roast (slow cooker).** Chuck roast, onion, garlic, bone broth, 8 h low; serves the week. ~50 g, ~4 g. 10 min hands-on. *Broth must pass the audit (no sugar, no yeast extract).*
8. **Shrimp & Zucchini Scampi.** Shrimp in butter/garlic, zucchini ribbons, parsley, lemon. ~38 g, ~6 g. 15 min.
9. **Turkey-Sage Meatballs + Marinara.** Ground turkey, egg, parmesan, sage; no-sugar marinara; over sautéed spinach. ~44 g, ~9 g. 25 min.

### Week 4 — "The repeat month starts here"
10. **Steak Salad, Baron style.** Sliced sirloin over romaine, blue cheese, bacon, tomato, olive-oil vinaigrette. ~45 g, ~7 g. 20 min.
11. **Coconut Chicken Curry (no rice).** Thighs, coconut milk, curry powder, ginger, cauliflower florets. ~42 g, ~11 g. 25 min.
12. **Baked Cod with Brown Butter + Green Beans.** Cod, brown butter, capers, green beans blistered in the pan. ~40 g, ~6 g. 15 min.
