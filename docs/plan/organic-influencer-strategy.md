# Organic Influencer + Founder-Led Strategy

_Last updated: 2026-05-06_

## Thesis

We cannot win on paid acquisition. TJ has roughly $6K of runway and a $1M-net target. Paid CAC in men's health is $80–$300+ depending on channel, and the brand is unproven. Burning runway on Meta or Google before the first cohort graduates is a fast way to die.

The only viable path is **organic, founder-led, brain-health-first**:

1. TJ writes one essay per week and syndicates it.
2. TJ pitches and lands podcast appearances in adjacent audiences (longevity, men's optimization, executive performance).
3. We pick communities where men 35–65 already gather and show up consistently.

The wedge is the angle no one else owns: cognitive longevity through final years, "begin with the end in mind," and the "eliminate the insulting behavior" frame. Weight loss, TRT, and GLP-1 are saturated. Brain-as-destination is not.

## Founder essay cadence

One essay per week. Publish on **my4mlife.com/blog** (canonical), syndicate to **Substack, LinkedIn, Medium** within 48 hours. Repurpose every essay as: 1 LinkedIn post, 3 X/Threads posts, 1 short-form video script.

Starter essay topics (all brain-health-anchored):

1. "The cognitive slide starts a decade before anyone names it"
2. "Begin with the end in mind: what your last decade actually requires"
3. "Eliminate the insulting behavior — the four things stealing your mind right now"
4. "Why I stopped treating the gut and the brain as separate problems"
5. "What 30 years of practice taught me about the 50-year-old executive"

7. "Hormone optimization is necessary. It is not sufficient."
8. "The granddaughter's wedding test: the only outcome that matters"
9. "Why visceral fat is a neurotoxin, not a vanity issue"
10. "What the longevity industry gets wrong about men who want to stay sharp"

## Adjacency targets

Communities where men 35–65 already gather around cognition or purpose:

1. **Vistage / YPO / EO chapters** — executive peer groups, brain-as-edge framing lands.
2. **BJJ gyms with masters-division demos** — older grapplers care about cognitive sharpness as much as joint health.
3. **Chess clubs / chess.com communities** — explicit cognitive-performance audience.
4. **Toastmasters chapters** — verbal fluency anxiety = early word-finding concern.
5. **Men's-circle / ManKind Project networks** — purpose-aligned, receptive to "begin with the end in mind."
6. **Endurance running/cycling clubs (50+)** — already optimization-minded, brain angle is fresh.
7. **Veteran transition groups** — cognitive resilience post-service is underserved.
8. **Father-of-teen-girls forums / "girl dad" communities** — granddaughter's-wedding framing resonates earlier than people think.

Light touch: one offer of value (free essay PDF, free Discovery audit) per community per quarter. No spamming.

## Tracking

Mirror the `affiliate-punch-list.csv` pattern. Single sheet, columns:

- `target_name` (podcast or community)
- `tier` (A / B / C / adjacency)
- `host_or_contact`
- `contact_method` (email / form / DM)
- `pitch_sent_date`
- `status` (queued / sent / replied / booked / declined / aired)
- `air_or_event_date`
- `url` (episode or community link once live)
- `notes`

Build the sheet when the first 5 pitches are ready to send — not before. Tracking infrastructure ahead of activity is a procrastination trap.
