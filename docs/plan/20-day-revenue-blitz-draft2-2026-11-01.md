## What changed from Draft 1 (and why)
1. **6 hours a day, not 10.** About 120 hours in all, so a quarter of Draft 1 is gone. Everything left is ranked by dollars per hour.
2. **Captain's Table is the main event.** It's about 75% of realistic November revenue for about 30 hours. It sells in small, trusted rooms, never from a stage.
3. **New: "Captain's Day" ($7,500).** One in-home day with you, fully credited toward the Table if they upgrade within 30 days. It gives every affluent "not now" a yes. *(Your call on the price.)*
4. **Partners: sign fewer, activate more.** The goal is 12–15 signed and 5 actually selling. That means a staff huddle plus one "Patch Night" at the best 3, and each partner sending one endorsed message to their own client list. Jay Abraham's host-beneficiary model: their list, their trust, our product.
5. **Rule of 100 (Hormozi), adjusted.** 50 personal touches a day (texts, voice memos, calls) to your warm list, each ending with a booked next step or "who do you know?" First we merge your phone, Facebook, LinkedIn and Instinct contacts into one list.
6. **Events: volunteer first, teach, never sell.** Collect contacts with the free Brain Check and follow up within 48 hours.
7. **Media capped at about 6 hours.** It pays off in January more than November, so: one ready-made Thanksgiving segment pitch plus daily expert-quote replies Claude drafts.
8. **The ranch "dinner" becomes a ranch lunch** (11:30–2:30), so it fits your window.

## Traps we're avoiding (from the research)
- Building instead of selling. Nothing new gets built unless it closes money this month.
- Signing partners who never sell. Activate 5 instead of collecting 25.
- Pitching the $50K seat from a stage or at lunch. Book a one-on-one instead.
- Cold blasts, bought lists, fake or "typical result" testimonials, mass texting. Texts go 1:1 from your phone.
- Counting podcasts as November revenue. They're January's pipeline.
- Trying to close anything after Nov 20. Everyone goes quiet at Thanksgiving.
