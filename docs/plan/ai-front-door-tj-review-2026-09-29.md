### 17. Why does gut health matter for my brain?

Your gut isn't just digesting food. It's manufacturing the neurochemistry your brain runs on. About 90% of your body's serotonin — the neurotransmitter that governs mood, sleep, and appetite — is produced in your gut. About 50% of your dopamine, which drives motivation and focus, is made there too. Roughly half your norepinephrine, which keeps you alert, originates in gut-adjacent organs.

The vagus nerve is the highway between them. About 80% of the signaling goes gut-to-brain, not the other way around. Your gut is talking to your head far more than your head is talking back.

When your gut lining becomes inflamed or permeable, that inflammation travels to your brain. It shows up in your cognitive score before it shows up anywhere else, which is why the protocol starts in the gut. This is one of the most underestimated drivers of early brain aging.
