# Speaking-Circuit Traffic Strategy — Book as the Trojan Horse

_Drafted 2026-07-21 (rebuild — original brainstorm lost in account snafu). $0 budget. Goal: first sales + site traffic starting this week._

## Thesis

TJ gives free educational talks on cognitive-decline prevention to audiences already worried about it. The book (*Begin with the End in Mind*) is the Trojan horse: it makes him "the author," not "a salesman." The talk gives away genuinely valuable prevention advice (broad strokes of the 4Ms), and the only ask is a QR code to the **free MindSpan assessment** — which IS the Protégé signup funnel (assessment → email with top-3 + book + workbook + app → gut-repair purchase path). Nothing is sold from the stage; everything is sold by the funnel.

This works because:
- Zero cash cost. Venues below actively look for free speakers.
- The audience self-selects: anyone who shows up to a "protect your memory" talk is pre-qualified.
- A talk converts at in-person trust levels no ad can reach, and TJ's 30 years of clinical pattern stories are the content.
- Every talk is also content: record on a phone → clips for LinkedIn/YouTube → podcast-pitch credibility.

## The talk (one talk, refined forever)

**Working title:** "The 10-Year Warning: how cognitive decline announces itself a decade early — and what you can do about it, starting today."

**Shape (40 min + Q&A):**
1. The fear, named out loud (identity + dignity — homepage language).
2. The 10-year warning signs (word-finding, shallow sleep, workouts feel like chores, the bedroom goes quiet first — ED-canary thesis, calibrated for a mixed audience).
3. The stat: 99% of Americans value brain health equally to physical health; only 9% know a lot about maintaining it (Alzheimer's Association 2026, attributed).
4. The gut-brain axis in plain language (~90% of serotonin, half of dopamine; the damaged-lining → inflammation → fog cascade).
5. The 4Ms as the broad-strokes framework — genuinely teach it, hold nothing critical back.
6. Close: "Begin with the end in mind" + the granddaughter's-wedding test.
7. The only CTA: QR code → my4mlife.com/assessment ("7 minutes, free, you'll get your score, your top-3 priorities, and my book").

**Q&A is the sales floor.** Personal questions get "that's exactly what the assessment sorts out — scan the code and the email walks you through it."

## Venue targets (ranked by bookability this week)

**Tier 1 — book within days, speak within 1–3 weeks:**
- **Rotary / Kiwanis / Lions / Optimist clubs** — they need a speaker EVERY WEEK, 20–30 min slot is their standard format, and the program chair's email is on the club website. Highest-probability first booking.
- **Public libraries (adult programming)** — health-literacy talks are exactly what programming coordinators want; free room, they do the promotion.
- **Senior centers & 55+ community activity directors** — audiences literally organized around this fear; activity directors book months of programming and answer email fast.
- **Churches — men's groups / adult education nights** — men-first audience, mission-compatible framing.

**Tier 2 — book within 2–4 weeks:**
- **Osher Lifelong Learning Institutes (OLLI)** at nearby universities — educated 50+ audiences who pay to attend lectures; being an OLLI presenter is also a credibility line.
- **Alzheimer's Association local chapter** educational events (per docs/research/alzheimers-association-alignment.md — clinician presentations are the achievable tier). Also: caregiver support groups — attendees are adult children worried about *themselves* next.
- **Chambers of commerce / business networking breakfasts** — peak-power demographic in the room; "executive cognitive performance" framing.
- **VFW / American Legion / Elks** — older male audiences, always need programs.
- **YMCA / country clubs / golf clubs** — wellness programming slots.

**Tier 3 — multiplier venues:**
- **Financial advisors, estate attorneys, CPAs** hosting client-appreciation seminars — their clients are planning for the end of life already; "protect the mind that signs the documents" is a perfect co-hosted event, and the advisor pays for the room/coffee (still $0 to us).
- **Corporate lunch-and-learns** — doubles as the corporate-wellness-vertical wedge (docs/plan/corporate-wellness-vertical-draft.md).
- **Podcasts** — 20 pitches already written and ready in docs/launch/podcast-pitches/podcast-pitch-batch-1.md.
