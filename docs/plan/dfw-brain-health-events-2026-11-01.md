# DFW brain-health event calendar: volunteer, educate, meet the donors (2026-11-01)

**Status: RESEARCH ONLY.** Nothing has been registered, submitted, emailed, or signed. Everything here is public information from the organizer's own page where possible.

**Verification key:**
- **VERIFIED** means the organizer's own page shows this date or edition.
- **LISTED** means a credible third-party listing or a save-the-date shows it, but the organizer's registration page doesn't yet.
- **UNVERIFIED (pattern)** means no 2027 date is published, so the date is projected from past years. Confirm before you calendar it.

**Why this doc exists:** The Captain's Table needs about 2 closes a month, which means 10–20 qualified conversations. The buyer is often an affluent adult child who has watched a parent decline. Dementia and brain-health charity rooms are where that person already volunteers, sponsors, and sits at tables. This doc is the event layer under [captains-table-channel-map-2026-10-31.md](captains-table-channel-map-2026-10-31.md). Entries that are already rows in that map are cross-referenced as "Map #N" and not repeated in depth.

**Contact collection on the site:** the free Brain Check (`/assessment`) and the Uninsured Decade calculator (`/go/uninsured-decade`). Use one QR per room, tagged `?src=ev-<slug>`.

## 5. How to show up

1. **Volunteer first.** At every charity event TJ is a volunteer, committee member, or guest, never a vendor. Work the Promise Garden, the Thank You Brigade, or a committee. Donors notice who shows up to work.
2. **Educate, don't sell.** At charity events, no Captain's Table talk, no product, no brochures. If asked "what do you do?":
   > "I'm Dr. TJ. I help people lower their odds of what we're all walking for. I wrote *Begin with the End in Mind*."

   Lead with the middle-age-disease line: *Alzheimer's is a disease of middle age that presents when we're older.*
3. **Collect contacts by giving something free, and only when asked.** Offer one of two things:
   - The free **Brain Check** (`/assessment?src=ev-<slug>`) for the worried adult child.
   - The **Uninsured Decade calculator** (`/go/uninsured-decade?src=ev-<slug>`) for the owner or advisor.

   Carry one QR card, not a stack of flyers. Every entry goes to the single Contact list.
4. **Booths only where booths are expected:** the Empowering Seniors Expo (Y18), the Healthy Lives Matter seminar (Y16), and the self-hosted Longest Day (Y15).
5. **Follow up within 48 hours,** personally, referencing the conversation. Send a signed book to the two or three people who matter. The Captain's Table comes up only if they raise their own parent or their own fear. Then: "I take four people a year, by application. Here's the page."
6. **Respect the room.** No use of organization logos and no "partner of" claims. Never approach anyone inside a support group or memory café about services.
