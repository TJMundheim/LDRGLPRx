# Count Yourself Skinny — Standalone $1M/yr Profit Model (2026-08-19)

TJ directive: CYS (book + app) must stand alone as a revenue/profit entity. The My4MLife
cohort funnel is UPSIDE, not the model. Two largely distinct markets: low-hurdle counters
vs. 12-week voyage cohort members; many CYS users will never convert — that's fine.

## Why $1M profit is plausible
Near-zero overhead: $99/yr Apple fee, KDP print-on-demand, local-first app (no servers,
no PHI, no support burden at v1 scope). Marginal cost per user ≈ $0 → profit ≈ revenue.
The binding constraint is REACH, not cost.

## Four revenue engines (year-2 shape)
| Engine | Math | Profit |
|---|---|---|
| App Pro subs | 400K downloads × 5% × ~$15 net avg | ~$300K |
| Book retail (KDP + racks) | 40K copies × ~$5 margin | ~$200K |
| Book bulk direct | 60K copies × ~$4 (employers/clinics/churches/MA plans) | ~$240K |
| Employer Pro seats | 25 accounts × 800 seats × $12/yr | ~$240K |

B2B chunks (rows 3–4) are the realistic backbone: no PHI / no medical claims / no BAA
makes procurement trivial. Reuses corporate-wellness draft + $0-PEPM wedge thinking,
but CYS is the WHOLE product here, not a telehealth door.

## Free-PR machine
- "Your AirPods count your chews" = tech-health novelty story (app reviews, morning TV,
  TikTok demo format). v1.5 AirPods feature IS the marketing department.
- Gimmick co-marketing: "32 Challenge", chew-day tie-ins, earbud-brand bundles.
- Grocery racks (TJ idea 2026-08-19): free-standing rack slots (real-estate-guide model)
  = $2–4/copy offline CAC that also earns book margin. Checkout-aisle retail via
  Readerlink/IPS once sales traction exists.

## One-month-free mechanic (locked direction)
Book code = 1 free month of Pro + lock $14.99/yr annual if subscribed before the month
ends (urgency → annual conversion). Costs $0 marginal; raises perceived book value.

## Operational changes
1. Standalone brand posture: own one-page site + App Store link; own P&L; no My4MLife
   dependency in-product (ch.13 hand-off stays as free upside). Separate LLC = later, if earned.
2. App retention must work cohort-free: streak, trends, annual recap.
3. Highest-leverage sales asset: one-page employer/clinic offer sheet
   ("$6/employee, no PHI, no integration, live tomorrow") + KDP bulk channel.

## Honest constraint
Consumer sub line needs ~400K downloads — only happens if PR/virality fires. Sequence
accordingly: ship v1 + book, then v1.5 AirPods = the PR moment, then B2B outreach with
traction numbers.
