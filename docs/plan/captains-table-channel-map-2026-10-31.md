## Ground rules for every channel

1. **No referral fees to licensed professionals, ever.** The Captain's Table includes labs and physician-approved regenerative therapy, so paying a physician, nurse, or any licensed health professional for a referral is off the table. Texas's patient-solicitation statute reaches beyond federal programs. **The clean default is no paid referrals from anyone.** Partners are *introducers* and *educators*. They get value from us in other ways: a free talk for their clients, a book on every seat, co-hosted education, and making them look good. (This matches the standing "endorse the professional partner" rule.)
2. **Lead with education, never the offer.** Every room gets the talk (*The Uninsured Decade* for money rooms, *Begin with the End in Mind* / middle-age-disease framing for health and faith rooms). The Captain's Table is never pitched from a stage. It is mentioned once ("I take four people a year into a done-for-you program, by application") and lives at /coaching.
3. **One QR per room**, tagged `?src=ct-<slug>`, so we learn which rooms produce applications.
4. **Branding:** "Dr. TJ" only. No ", DC" on any educator or speaker surface.

## How the ranking works

**Score = Buyer density (1–5) × Ease of a warm intro for a Fort Worth doctor-author with $0 (1–5).** Max 25. Ease covers whether TJ already has a relationship, whether the room books outside speakers, and whether it costs money to get in.
