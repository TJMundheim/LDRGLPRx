## 1. The member journey (redefined) [TJ]

| Stage | Timeline | Role | Commission status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Protégé** | Weeks 1–12 | In the program. Free tier as today. | Not commission-eligible |
| **Ambassador** | Weeks 13–52 | Finished the voyage. Can refer and earn. | Commission-qualified from month one of Ambassador **[TJ]** |
| **Graduate** | Year 1+ | The earned senior status. | Commission rate bumps up and stays **[TJ]** |

The program itself is the entry gate: you cannot buy in, you have to have sailed the
12 weeks. No sign-up fee, no starter kit, no inventory — ever.

## 2. The qualifier [TJ — locked concept]

- To be commission-qualified, an Ambassador or Graduate must hold **at least one
  active product subscription** (any SKU — Biome NS Ultra now; MitoVita, OmegaCN
  Prime, SleepRestore, etc. as they ship).
- Cancel a subscription and **replace it with another** → still qualified, no gap.
- Cancel and don't replace → **drops out of the commission model.**
- This doubles as the authenticity guarantee: every person selling the products is
  taking the products. "Our salesforce is our graduates, and they're all on protocol."

**Proposed mechanics for TJ to decide (§8, Q1):** what happens to their referred
book while unqualified — pause vs. release.

## 3. Commission structure (proposed — react to these numbers)

| | Ambassador (wk 13–52) | Graduate (yr 1+) |
|---|---|---|
| Recurring % on personally referred **OTC subscription** revenue | **25%** | **30%** |
| One-time OTC orders (non-subscription) | 15% | 15% |
| Rx fills, consult fees, lab fees | 0% — excluded by design | 0% |

- Residuals run **for the life of the referred subscription**, as long as the
  ambassador stays qualified. That's the headline of the offer: *recurring income
  on people you helped, for as long as they stay healthy with us.*
- Rx/consults are never commissioned — but ambassador referrals land in the same
  funnel where the consult and Rx upsells already live, so the company keeps 100%
  of that margin on traffic the ambassador generated. (This exclusion is also what
  keeps physicians' prescribing decisions and our comp plan in separate rooms.)
- **Single-tier override (future, HOLD):** a Graduate who brings in ambassadors
  may later earn a modest override (proposed 5%) on those ambassadors' sales —
  one level only, never deeper, and only if Phase-1 data shows recruiters
  recruiting. Same subscription qualifier applies **[TJ]**. Not in launch scope.

### Worked scenario (TJ 2026-07-14 — confirms one-level design)

Ambassador A refers Protégés P1 and B. Both subscribe → A earns 25% on each,
monthly. B finishes his 12 weeks, becomes an Ambassador (subscribed = qualified),
and refers C1 and C2:
- **A keeps earning 25% on B's own autoship for as long as B subscribes** — B is
  A's direct referral; that residual can't churn while B stays qualified.
- **C1 and C2 pay B** (25%), not A. When C1 later becomes an ambassador and
  refers people, those pay C1. **Nothing ever deepens past one level, for anyone.**
- The only possible exception is the phase-3 Graduate override above (A earning
  ~5% on B's book) — HELD until data shows ambassadors recruiting ambassadors.
  Margin note: override on = 30% total payout on those sales (35% if B is a
  Graduate) — must clear product gross margin before enabling.

FAQ sentence for launch: *"When your referral becomes an ambassador, you keep
earning on his subscription for as long as he's in — but his customers are his."*

## 4. Attribution & payout rules (proposed)

- Each ambassador gets a **personal code + link** (e.g. my4mlife.com/go/gut-repair?ref=CODE and CODE at checkout).
- **Last touch wins**, 90-day attribution window; a subscription belongs to the
  referring ambassador for its lifetime once attributed.
- Self-referral is fine — their own subscription can use their code (it's the
  qualifier anyway); no commission on their own orders.
- Paid **monthly**, on cash actually collected the prior month (refunds/chargebacks
  claw back automatically). $25 minimum payout threshold, balance rolls forward.
  1099 issued at $600+/yr.
- Chargeback abuse = same lifetime-ban rule as members.

## 5. Live events [TJ — placeholder]

## 6. The AI back office (Phase 2 — what makes this "high touch + high tech")

Every ambassador gets, at zero marginal cost to us:
- An **approved content library** — claims-checked posts, scripts, and email/text
  copy in the Dr. TJ voice. Ambassadors share from the library; they don't
  freelance health claims. (The AI concierge is the compliance rail.)
- **AI-drafted follow-ups** for their referred contacts.
- A personal **/go/-style landing page** with their code baked in.
- A dashboard: referrals, active subscriptions, monthly residual, qualification
  status ("You are commission-qualified ✓ — subscription active").

This replaces everything a classic upline was paid to do — which is why the whole
commission pool goes to the person who made the sale.

## 7. Phased rollout

1. **Phase 1 — Founding Ambassadors (invite-only, first cohort).** Codes, attribution
   in the order handler, commission ledger, monthly payout. Announce during the
   cohort: *"Finish the voyage, stay on protocol, and you're a Founding Ambassador"*
   — upside stacked on top of accountability.
2. **Phase 2 — AI back office** (content library, dashboard, personal LPs) once
   there are ≥10 active ambassadors.
3. **Phase 3 — Graduate override tier**, only if data shows recruiters recruiting.
