## 1. The offer [TJ]

Dr. TJ personally takes on **three clients — never more** — for a total life
restructure around the 4Ms.

| Term | Price | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| 6 months (minimum) | **$60,000 prepaid** | One week per month on-site + full remote stack |
| 12 months | **$100,000 prepaid** | Same cadence; effectively 2 months free |

Anchor: Tony Robbins charges ~$1M/year for comparable personal-access coaching.
This is founder access at a tenth of that — and there are only three seats on earth.

Eligibility: open to Protégés now; down the road the natural pipeline is
Ambassadors and Graduates **[TJ]**. No assessment gate — the check is the gate —
but every client starts with the full MindSpan baseline and the app like everyone else.

## 2. Naming — "Overachievers" placeholder [TJ asked for better]

Recommendation: **The Captain's Table** — three seats.
- Native to the locked Odyssey/voyage theme (nautical, not Greek cosplay): the
  captain doesn't dine with everyone.
- Literal: a table seats a small number, and much of the on-site week happens
  in the client's kitchen — cooking, purging, rebuilding. The name is the perk.
- Ties directly to the Keto Cattle Baron content engine (§5).
- Members are "seat-holders"; a seat "opens" only when someone graduates off.

Alternates considered: The Wheelhouse (with the captain at the helm), Flagship
Three (pairs with Biome NS "flagship product" language — possibly confusing),
First Mate Program (sounds subordinate). "Overachievers" reads corporate-seminar,
not voyage.

## 3. The on-site cadence [TJ — revised 2026-07-14]

Price unchanged. Visit shape (replaces the original "one full week per month"):

- **Months 1–3: Sunday–Tuesday on-site, fly out Wednesday.** Sunday starts
  early with the **Captain's Table cooking broadcast shoot** — which doubles
  as the client's actual meal prep for the entire week. Monday–Tuesday: the
  restructure work (§ below).
- **Months 4–6: same Sunday–Tuesday footprint**, evolving outward — the Sunday
  shoot becomes meal-prep content for the **whole cohort**, broadcast from the
  seat-holder's kitchen, and the coaching becomes concierge-grade 1:1 across
  any and all elements of the 4Ms as the household systems (already installed
  in months 1–3) shift to maintenance.

**Why this works (Fable):**
- **Capacity solved.** 3 clients × ~3.5 days = **~10 travel days/month** (was
  ~21). Three Sun→Wed trips, home Wednesday–Saturday every week, plus one
  fully clear week per month for the company.
- **It locks onto the existing meal rhythm.** The locked cohort workflow is
  Wednesday picks-email → Saturday shop → **Sunday cook**. The Table's Sunday
  shoot IS that Sunday cook — meaning the whole cohort's cook-along can be
  hosted live from a seat-holder's kitchen. The premium tier literally
  produces the free tier's programming.
- **Sunday-early start earns the short visit.** The client gets the highest-
  value day (full meal prep for their week, on camera, with the founder at
  their stove) plus two full working days — the "week per month" promise
  becomes "your week runs on what we built Sunday."

### What happens on-site

Every part of the book, applied to their actual life, in their actual house:

1. **Kitchen & pantry purge** — eliminate the mitigating, insulting foods
   shelf-by-shelf; restock via the vetted affiliate cart (ButcherBox, Thrive,
   the /weekly-picks backbone). The "eliminate the insulting behavior" chapter,
   performed live.
2. **Environmental audit of the whole house** — bedroom darkening, red-light
   zones for nighttime, morning-light plan, water filtration, air, EMF hot
   spots. The Environment pillar, installed rather than described.
3. **Movement & protocol setup** — equipment placed (pull-up bar, wedges, BP
   cuff, pulse ox), fasted-walk route walked together, strength sessions
   coached in their space.
4. **The YouTube shoot** [TJ] — that month's **Keto Cattle Baron weekly
   meal-prep episode is filmed in their kitchen, with them in the show** (see
   §5). Their transformation becomes serialized content — with their consent
   and star billing.
5. **The sit-down** — accountability-target work (the specific face), the
   quarter's goals, the wife/family brought into the protocol where they want in.

**Capacity flag: RESOLVED 2026-07-14** by the Sunday–Tuesday cadence above
(~10 travel days/month at full capacity, down from ~21).

## 4. The remote stack (the other three weeks)

- **24-hour access** [TJ] — structured as: AI concierge first line around the
  clock (it knows their protocol, their data, their history), **Dr. TJ
  personally within hours, and immediately for anything urgent.** This keeps
  the promise honest at 3 clients without TJ's phone owning his sleep.
- Weekly 1:1 video call; direct text line.
- Full product stack included — every My4MLife SKU, auto-shipped (they are, by
  definition, subscription-qualified for the ambassador track).
- Labs + telemedicine coordination through the care team (clinical work stays
  with the licensed physicians — TJ coaches the lifestyle, the partners
  practice the medicine).
- **Priority access + standing invitation to every live and remote event**,
  including seat-holder-only sessions with the other members of the Table [TJ].
- Spouse/partner included in everything at no charge (the accountability-target
  research says this doubles adherence — and it's the right story).

## 5. Why this is more than revenue — the content engine [TJ]

The Keto Cattle Baron YouTube channel needs a weekly meal-prep episode that
drives affiliate carts (ButcherBox, Thrive, et al. → hosted tagged-links page,
same ToS-safe pattern as /weekly-picks). The Captain's Table supplies:
- **A location and co-star every on-site week** — 3 clients = up to 3 episodes
  a month shot on location, real kitchens, real men, real restocked pantries.
- **A longitudinal transformation arc** — each client's MindSpan score
  re-tested on camera over 6–12 months. That's a series, not a clip.
- **The proof layer the whole brand needs** — three documented, named,
  filmed transformations become the case studies for every tier below
  (with consent handled in the contract, §6).

Every dollar of travel is double-spent: coaching delivery + content production.

## 6. Terms to decide (open for TJ)

1. **Refunds on prepaid:** recommend non-refundable after the first on-site
   week, with a "first-week guarantee" (walk away within week one for a full
   refund minus travel). Clean, confident, protects both sides.
2. **Travel costs:** recommend all-inclusive within the continental US (the
   price supports it); international by arrangement.
3. **Media consent:** baked into the agreement — YouTube participation is a
   named perk, with a carve-out list the client controls (what's off-camera).
4. **Health screening:** a telemedicine intake before acceptance — TJ's
   on-site coaching pairs with a physician-reviewed protocol from day one.
5. **Renewal:** seat-holders get first right to renew before a seat opens
   publicly. A waitlist ("the manifest") builds scarcity from day one.
6. **Start dates:** stagger the three clients across weeks 1/2/3 of each month
   (Sun–Tue each), leaving week 4 fully at HQ. Every cohort Sunday cook-along
   then rotates between three real kitchens.

## 7. Fit with the rest of the architecture

  The Captain's Table as the invitation-only apex available from any stage.

- **Revenue:** 3 × $100K = $300K/yr prepaid cash at near-zero COGS beyond
