This Partner Agreement (this "Agreement") is between My 4M Life ("My 4M Life," "we," or "us") and {{BUSINESS}} ("Partner" or "you"). It explains how you share the Push Patch with your clients, how you get paid, and what each of us agrees to. We have kept it short and plain on purpose.

## 1. The Program

The Push Patch is a prescription product. Every order is reviewed by a licensed physician in My 4M Life's network, who may decline it. My 4M Life handles checkout, physician review, fulfillment and shipping. You tell your clients about the Push Patch and give them your link or code. You never handle the product, and you never handle your clients' health information.

## 2. Your Link, Code and Client Offer

- **Your link:** https://my4mlife.com/go/push-patch?ref={{PARTNER_CODE}}
- **Client offer:** {{CLIENT_OFFER}} the client's first Push Patch order
- **Your commission:** {{COMMISSION}} per qualifying order

The client offer is applied automatically when a client uses your link or enters your code at checkout.

## 3. Commission

You earn {{COMMISSION}} for each approved, paid order placed through your link or code. An order qualifies once the physician has approved it and the client's payment has been collected.

- **Declined orders.** A declined order is refunded to the client and earns no commission.
- **Refunds and chargebacks.** If an order is refunded or charged back within 30 days after payment, no commission is owed on it. If we have already paid you for that order, we will subtract it from your next payout.
- **Your clients stay yours.** A client who first orders through your link or code stays attributed to you for future orders, including refills, while this Agreement is in effect.
- **Changes.** We may change commission terms for future orders with 30 days' written notice. Changes never reduce commissions already earned.

## 4. Tracking and Statements

Each order is credited to the partner whose link or code is on the paid order. Stripe's payment records are the official record for orders, refunds and commissions. We will email you a monthly statement listing your orders and the commission on each. If you think something is missing or wrong, tell us within 60 days of the statement date and we will review it in good faith.

## 5. Getting Paid

We pay commissions monthly, by the 15th, for the prior calendar month. There is no minimum payout. Payments are made through Stripe. Before your first payout, you complete Stripe's secure onboarding, which includes your W-9 and bank details. Your tax ID goes to Stripe, and My 4M Life never stores it.

## 6. How You Promote the Push Patch

You agree to:

- use only marketing materials and wording that My 4M Life provides or approves in writing;
- let clients know you earn a commission when you recommend the Push Patch (an FTC rule for endorsements);
- make no medical, treatment or cure claims, and give no medical advice about the Push Patch (send clinical questions to us);
- not collect, store or pass along any client health information for this program;
- send no spam: follow CAN-SPAM for email and the TCPA for texts and calls, and contact only people who have agreed to hear from you;
- not run paid ads (search, social or otherwise) that use the My 4M Life or Push Patch names or trademarks without our written OK;
- offer no discount beyond your client offer, and no cash, gift or other reward to clients for ordering; and
- follow all laws that apply to your business and your promotion of the program.

## 7. Our Relationship

You are an independent contractor. Neither of us is the other's employee, agent, partner or joint venturer, and neither of us can make promises or sign anything for the other. This Agreement is not exclusive either way: you may promote other products, and we may work with other partners.

## 8. Taxes

You are responsible for your own taxes on commissions you receive. You provide your W-9 through Stripe. We (or Stripe for us) will issue a Form 1099-NEC when required by law.

## 9. Term and Termination

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until either of us ends it with 30 days' written notice. Email is fine. Either of us may end it immediately if the other breaks it. Commissions earned before the end date are still paid on the normal schedule, subject to Section 3. Sections 3 (refunds and chargebacks), 8, 10 and 11 continue after the Agreement ends.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each of us will keep private any non-public information the other shares under this Agreement, such as pricing, statements and business plans. Each of us will use it only for this program, unless the law requires its disclosure.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits. My 4M Life's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the commissions owed to you under it.

Any change to it must be in writing (email is fine), except commission changes made under Section 3. You may not transfer this Agreement without our written OK.
