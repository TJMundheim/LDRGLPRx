Section 7 — Your Rights
A. Right to Revoke
You have the right to revoke this Authorization at any time by submitting a written revocation to My 4M Life's Privacy Officer at the address listed in our Notice of Privacy Practices. Revocation will not apply to information that has already been used or disclosed in reliance on this Authorization.
B. Conditioning of Treatment
We may not condition your treatment, payment, enrollment in a health plan, or eligibility for benefits on whether you sign this Authorization, except as permitted under 45 CFR § 164.508(b)(4) (e.g., research-related treatment, or services created solely for the purpose of disclosure to a third party).
C. Potential for Re-disclosure
Information used or disclosed pursuant to this Authorization may be subject to re-disclosure by the recipient and may no longer be protected by federal privacy regulations. Special protections may continue to apply to substance use disorder records (42 CFR Part 2), HIV/AIDS, mental health, and genetic information under federal or state law.
D. Right to a Copy
You are entitled to a copy of this signed Authorization.
