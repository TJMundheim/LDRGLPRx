## Description (1,228 chars incl. tags, plain HTML allowed by KDP)

<p><b>This is not a book to read. It is a logbook to run.</b></p>
<p>The My4MLife Logbook is the write-in companion to <i>Begin with the End in Mind</i>. It lays out the first four weeks of the My4MLife protocol one page at a time, so you can move from reading about the plan to actually doing it, with a pen in your hand.</p>
<p><b>How to use it</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Start with Part 1: name the face you are doing this for, write your why, and set your baseline.</li>
<li>Run Weeks 1 through 4 in order. Each week has daily check-ins, a strength-session log, a weekly Zoom prep page, an end-of-week reflection and an adherence scorecard.</li>
<li>Week 3 includes a self-audit on hormones and sexual function, with separate worksheets for men and for women.</li>
<li>Week 4 closes with a full audit of the habits that work against you and a 30-day recommit procedure.</li>
<li>The stack reference at the back explains each item in the plan and why it is there.</li>
</ul>
<p>Write in it. Mark it up. The order is the order on purpose.</p>
<p><i>Don't lose your identity and your dignity while you still have a choice.</i></p>
<p>Educational and personal-tracking tool. It is not a substitute for care from your own physician.</p>
