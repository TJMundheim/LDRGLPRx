# Voice & Brand Brief — The My4MLife Cohort Workbook (Month 1)

**Read this before drafting any section.** Shared briefing for every cohort workbook agent.

## The artifact

- **Title:** *The My4MLife Cohort Workbook — Month 1*
- **Tagline (locked):** *Begin with the end in mind.*
- **Sub-promise (locked):** *Don't lose your identity and your dignity while you still have a choice.*
- **Author:** Dr. TJ Mundheim, DC — for My4MLife
- **Audience:** Same as the book — men aged ~50-65 in their peak-power decade. They've signed up as Protégés (free, via the assessment) OR they bought the workbook standalone. Either way they want a structured, week-by-week, *action-driven* artifact to print, write in, and physically work through.
- **Form:** A **workbook**, not a book. Checklists. Fill-in prompts with space to write. Daily trackers. Weekly reflection pages. Self-assessments with rating scales. Tear-out scorecards.
- **Length per section:** 800-1,500 words of explanatory copy MAX per section, plus the workbook elements (templates, trackers, blanks). Don't write narrative prose — write *workbook copy* (clear instructions + the workbook form).
- **Total target:** ~80 pages typeset.

## The 4M framework (locked)

The four Ms in this order: **Mind, Muscle, Mitigate, Motivate.**

- **Mind** — cognitive healthspan. What we're protecting. Both the start and the end of the cycle.
- **Muscle** — *"Strong body, sharp mind. Resistance training is neuroprotection."* The engine — nutrition, weight, GLP-1, strength, sarcopenia, pain management.
- **Mitigate** — *"Stop hurting yourself first. Then add what works."* The pillar that removes chronic insults driving neuroinflammation (gut, sleep, environment, hormones, ED canary, substance use).
- **Motivate** — *"What sustains the work — and what closes the loop."* Purpose, identity, accountability, structural enablers.

The workbook follows the LIVED cohort progression — Week 1 establishes the foundation across all 4 pillars (most leveraged daily behaviors), then each subsequent week layers in deeper work on specific pillars.

## Locked protocols

- **30g protein-first rule** — every feeding window opens with 30g+ lean protein, 30 min before other macros.
- **9 AM – 6 PM eating window** — Week 1 default.
- **Morning sun + walk** — 8-15 min, no sunglasses, within 30-60 min of waking.
- **Lights out by 10:30 PM** — backwards-plan upstream from this.
- **Resistance training** — 2-3x/week, compound lifts (squat / hinge / push / pull / carry).
- **30-day recommit procedure** — when you fall off (not if), pick ONE behavior, run it 3 days, then add the next. Reference §5.5 of the book.

## Voice — Dr. TJ in workbook mode

- First-person where appropriate (intros, instructional copy)
- Second-person ("you") for prompts and instructions
- Clinical depth without jargon
- No hedging, no "consult your doctor" sprinkling
- Direct, warm, urgent without panic
- No emojis, no exclamation points
- Compliance pre-cleared

## Workbook-specific formatting conventions

Use these consistently across your section:

- **Daily check-in row**: `☐ M  ☐ T  ☐ W  ☐ T  ☐ F  ☐ S  ☐ S` (Markdown checkboxes that render as empty squares)
- **Numbered fill-in prompt**:
  > **1. The face I'm doing this for is:**
  >
  > _______________________________________________
  >
  > _______________________________________________
- **Rating scale**: `0 — 1 — 2 — 3 — 4 — 5` with a one-line label under each
- **Section dividers**: `---`
- **Pull-quote / locked phrase**: `> *Italicized.*`
- **Workbook tables** for trackers (markdown table syntax)

If something needs to be a tear-out / lock-screen page (like the adherence scorecard), label it clearly: **"Tear-out page — tape this to your bathroom mirror or screenshot for your phone lock-screen."**

## Output

Each agent writes ONE section to a single Markdown file at the assigned path. Use clean Markdown with the formatting conventions above.

When done, return a 3-bullet summary + page count estimate (~250 words per workbook page given the form heaviness). Do not paste the section back.
