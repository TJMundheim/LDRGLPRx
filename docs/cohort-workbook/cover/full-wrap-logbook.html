This is not a book to read.
It is a logbook to run.

The My4MLife Logbook is the write-in companion to Begin with the End in Mind: the first four weeks of the protocol, laid out one page at a time. You start by naming the face you are doing this for, writing your why, and setting your baseline.

Then you run the month. Each week has daily check-ins, a Wednesday Zoom prep page, an end-of-week reflection, and an adherence scorecard, with strength-session logs, a hormone and sexual-function self-audit, and a full eliminate-the-insulting-behavior audit along the way.

A stack reference at the back explains every item you are asked to take and why. Keep a pen in your hand. The order is the order on purpose.
