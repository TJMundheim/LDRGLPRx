## Tips on sending

- **Send to the BD/partnerships address**, not the generic info@ address. Look on their site → "Partners," "Sales," or "Contact" → BD email. If not visible, LinkedIn-search "[Company name] BD" or "[Company name] Partnerships."
- **Send before 10am their timezone** for highest open rate.
- **Follow up after 4 business days** with a one-liner: *"Bumping this up — any availability this week?"*
- **If they ask for volume projections**, give honest numbers: *"Conservative first-90-days projection is 50-200 consults/month, scaling with our content + organic growth strategy. We're cash-pay, no insurance billing."*
- **Don't reveal which other partners you're talking to** unless asked. Keep optionality.

## After you have 2-3 answers in hand

We compare:
- Minimum commitments
- Per-consult fees (often $40-80 to the platform; lab markup adds $20-50)
- Schedule III coverage by state
- Time-to-launch (how fast can they activate us)
- Brand handling (do they let "Dr. X via My4MLife" go on the consult email, or only "Dr. X")
- API roadmap (manual handoff at launch, API integration when volume justifies)
