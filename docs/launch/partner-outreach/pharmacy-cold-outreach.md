## Tips on sending

- **Send to the BD/sales address**, not the patient-fulfillment address. Look for "Wholesale," "Partnerships," "B2B," or "Business Development." If not visible, LinkedIn-search "[Pharmacy Name] business development" or "[Pharmacy Name] wholesale."
- **Empower specifically:** they have a partnerships team. Try `partnerships@empowerpharmacy.com` or look up the BD lead on LinkedIn.
- **If they ask for volume projections, give honest numbers:** *"Conservative first-90-days projection is 50-200 patients/month on at least one Rx SKU, scaling with our content strategy. We're cash-pay, no insurance routing — clean transactions."*
- **NDA-first is normal** for wholesale pricing disclosure. Don't be surprised when they ask. We can sign mutual NDAs with no committed minimums.

## After you have 2-3 answers in hand

We compare:
- Wholesale cost per SKU (the spread between their wholesale and our retail = our margin)
- Volume thresholds to unlock better tiers
- Payment terms (longer is better for our cash flow)
- Drop-ship SLA (how fast prescription → patient's door)
- Quality reputation (503A vs 503B facility, USP <797> compliance, third-party testing)
- Schedule III handling (testosterone shipping restrictions by state)
- Returns/recalls handling

I'll build the comparison matrix once you have the responses.

## Why this email reads the way it does (drafting notes — delete before sending)

- **Opening positioning earns the read** — "brain-health-first" + "Dr. TJ Mundheim" + 4M framework signals we're serious, not a fly-by-night TRT shop

- **Honest exit language** — "we'd value an honest no" — earns goodwill and gets faster responses (people respect candor)
