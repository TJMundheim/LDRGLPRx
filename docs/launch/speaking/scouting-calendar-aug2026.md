**Guest etiquette (every club below):** call or email a day ahead so they count you for the meal. Budget **$20–30 cash** for the lunch clubs, less for breakfast/evening. Dress as you would to see a patient. Business cards yes, books no — you're scouting. On the way out, ask the program chair one question: *"How do you line up your speakers?"* That's the whole mission.
