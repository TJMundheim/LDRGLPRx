## 1. Summary and the math

**The plan in one paragraph.** December stays light: the Ridglea Rotary visit (Tue Dec 1), 3–5 easy noon intros near Benbrook, and the email batches already planned (batch 2 + batch 3 on Mon Nov 30, batch 1 follow-ups Wed Dec 2). There's no big December phone campaign. **The real booking push starts the week of Jan 4–8**: about 20 calls, about 12 new emails or forms, and a New Year nudge to everyone who hasn't answered. Clubs book 4–12 weeks ahead, so **mid- and late January will be thin**. January gets filled with the rooms that book fast: employer lunch-and-learns, church classes, the Benbrook library, senior center and YMCA, the Heritage client night, chamber events, and your own hosted talk. The guest visits happen in January too. Club talks start landing in February, and March and April are where 3 a week becomes realistic.

**The target math**

| | Number |
|---|---|
| Weeks in the window (Jan 11 – Apr 16) | 14 |
| Goal: 3 talks a week | **42 talks** |
| Local civic/business groups with real dated meetings in the window | ~15 daytime + 5 early/evening ("TJ's choice") |
| Each club books you about once | so clubs give **~12–18 talks** at best |
| Left to fill from repeatable rooms (employers, churches, libraries, caregiver groups, your own talk) | **~25** |
| Realistic outcome if the weekly rhythm holds | **25–32 talks**. 42 needs the fill rooms to say yes and a few early/evening slots. |

**What that takes each week, starting Jan 4:**
- **15 new asks** (email or form): cold asks convert at about 1 in 5–10.
- **10 phone calls**: many groups have no email, and a call converts better.
- **1–2 guest visits**: go first, pitch after ("I visited on the 12th and heard your program").
- **1 "who else should hear this?"** at every talk. That's the warmest lead you'll get.
- **Follow-up at +12 days** on every ask that hasn't been answered.

Rough check: ~170 asks between Nov 30 and Mar 19 at about 25% blended yield ≈ 42 talks. December covers ~27 of those asks, so Jan 4 – Mar 19 needs ~12–15 new asks a week.

**Rules for every talk**
- You're "Dr. TJ" on every speaker surface (no ", DC").
- Every pitch opens with both doors: *"If you're helping a parent through this…"* / *"If you want to make sure it never happens to you…"*
- QR tag `talk-<slug>` (run `python3 docs/talk/qr.py talk-<slug>`) plus a talk-log row, wherever a QR is allowed.
- The **library edition** (Fort Worth Public Library) has no QR slide and no book table, and you ask before handing out the my4mlife.com/speaking link. The City contract must be executed first. At the Benbrook library: free, non-commercial, and ask whether the QR slide is OK.
- **Alzheimer's Association Community Educator** talks use their curriculum. No My4MLife, no QR, no books.
- One talk a day by default, inside 10–4. Noon is ideal. 7:45 am breakfasts and 6 pm meetings are **TJ's choice**: they're flagged below, not scheduled.
