## The math for 3 talks a week
- 3 talks/week ≈ 13 a month.
- Cold booking emails convert at maybe 10–20%, and clubs book 4–12 weeks out. So: **20 new booking emails every week**, plus follow-ups, plus one "who else should hear this?" ask at every talk.
- Warm routes convert far better: host referrals, Rotary/club members who are also in other groups, affiliates' client nights, advisors' client events.
- December 2026 is the build month: if bookings for January–February aren't landing by mid-December, increase the weekly sends.
