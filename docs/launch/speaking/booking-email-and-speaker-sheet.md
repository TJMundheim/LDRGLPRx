## One-page speaker sheet (copy — lay out later as PDF)

**DR. TJ MUNDHEIM** — NBCE-certified since 1994 · 30+ years in clinical practice · Fort Worth, TX
Author of *Begin with the End in Mind* · Founder of My4MLife

**Signature talk:**
### The 10-Year Warning
*How cognitive decline announces itself a decade early — and what you can do about it, starting today.*

Cognitive decline doesn't arrive overnight. In thirty years of clinical practice, Dr. TJ watched the same pattern repeat: word-finding gets slower, sleep gets shallower, workouts start feeling like chores — a full decade before anyone names what's happening. This talk teaches audiences to recognize the pattern early and act on it, built on the four-pillar "4M" framework from his book.

**Audiences learn:**
- The early warning signs most people rationalize away for ten years
- The gut-brain connection in plain English — where roughly 90% of serotonin is made, and why gut-lining health shows up as brain fog
- The 4M framework: Mind, Muscle, Mitigate, Motivate
- Three concrete steps to start this week — at any age

**Formats:** 20–25 min (service clubs) · 40 min + Q&A (libraries, centers, lunch-and-learns) · slides optional, no AV required
**Fee:** Free — community education. Nothing is sold from the stage.
**Book:** *Begin with the End in Mind* — available on Amazon (ebook, paperback, hardcover)
**Contact:** drtj@essentialmanage.com · [phone] · my4mlife.com

_Health education, not medical advice. Attendees are encouraged to work with their own physicians._
