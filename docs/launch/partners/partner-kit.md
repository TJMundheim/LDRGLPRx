**Who this is for.** IV bars and wellness clinics with NAD+ drips, NAD+ shots or memberships.

**The idea in one line.** Your client's drip is a peak, and the patch fills in the valley between visits. You keep the chair time, and you also earn on the week.

**Talking points for the owner**

- **It doesn't compete with the chair.** We never tell clients to stop their drips. The headline on your counter is "keep the drip."

- **It's zero inventory.** You don't stock it, ship it or prescribe it. Your client orders on their phone, and you earn $100–$150 per approved order.

**Where to put it**

- Mention it in the checkout script: "for the days in between."
- For a membership email or text, use only the approved wording from Section 5.

**Best blends to mention:** NAD+ Restore (the simplest start), Metabolic, and Wolverine or Repair for recovery clients.
