# Pull-Up Challenge: Men's and Women's Divisions (redline for Dr. TJ to approve)

Source: `brief-2026-11-12.md`, Parts 1 to 4 (TJ's wording, verbatim). Nothing here is live until TJ approves it. The website and lambda files are not touched by this document.

## What changes (summary)

1. Two divisions, Men's and Women's. Each has its own six-week Push Patch package (still $550+ each), so there are two winners in all.
2. Each division has a host who competes under the same formula. TJ hosts the men's. [HER NAME] ([HER @HANDLE]) hosts the women's.
3. You're scored only within your division: men against men and TJ, women against women and [HER NAME]. If a division's host beats that division's top challenger, no prize is awarded in that division.
4. The form gets one required field, "Division: Men's / Women's". Entrants pick the division that matches their sex, and an entry in the wrong division is moved to the right one.
5. Caption: the first line reports a true max ([N] reps), one line invites women to the women's division, and the stray word "Instinct" is removed.

Unchanged: dates, prize value per package, the formula, video requirements, the Meta release, and the rest of the posting steps.

**Markup:** `[+added+]` and `[-removed-]`. Brackets such as `[HER NAME]` are placeholders to fill in before publishing.

## Decisions for TJ

- **Tagging (recommended: the simplest option).** Every entrant tags @drtjmundheim, whatever their division. Women's entrants *may* also tag [HER @HANDLE]. Why: all entries are found and verified through the @drtjmundheim tag. That keeps one search, one rule, and the posting steps unchanged, and a missing optional tag can never disqualify anyone. [HER NAME]'s own videos tag @drtjmundheim and use both hashtags, just like everyone else's.
- **Choosing a division.** "Choose the division that matches your sex." It's one plain sentence with no proof asked for. *Sensitivity:* sex-based divisions with self-selection can draw questions, so legal may want a quick look at that sentence.
- **Placeholders to fill:** [HER NAME], [HER @HANDLE], [HER AGE], [HER WEIGHT], and [N] (TJ's new true-max count for the caption).
- **Her example uses TJ's 5-to-15 reps** so the two host examples match. Swap in her own numbers if you prefer. Worked illustration only: at age 45 and 140 lb, her multiplier is 1.15 and 10 x 140 x 1.15 = 1,610.
