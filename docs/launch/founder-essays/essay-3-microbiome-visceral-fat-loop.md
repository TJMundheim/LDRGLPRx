## Section headers + 1-2 sentence summaries

### 1. The loop, in plain English
Dysbiotic microbiome → intestinal permeability ("leaky gut") → LPS endotoxemia → systemic low-grade inflammation → insulin resistance → visceral fat accumulation → adipose-derived cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha) → blood-brain barrier permeability → neuroinflammation → cognitive decline. Each arrow is well-cited in the literature. The integrated picture almost never gets drawn.

### 2. Visceral fat is a neurotoxin, not a vanity issue
Visceral adipose tissue is endocrinologically active. It is not a passive storage depot — it is a cytokine factory. Men who carry it preferentially over the omentum are running a low-grade systemic inflammatory state that, year over year, degrades cerebrovascular and cognitive function. The mirror is the least interesting reason to address it.

### 3. Why single-symptom platforms structurally cannot fix this
A TRT-only clinic raises testosterone in a man whose visceral fat is still aromatizing it back to estradiol. A GLP-1-only clinic strips weight while leaving the dysbiosis intact and lean mass collapsing. A "gut health" subscription sends a probiotic into a system whose mucosal barrier is structurally compromised. None of these are wrong. All of them are insufficient on their own.

### 4. The 4M frame as the only honest answer
Mitigate the upstream drivers (food, glucose, sleep, environmental). Move to drive insulin sensitivity and lean mass. Motivate around the only outcome that actually matters — the granddaughter's-wedding test. Mind as destination. The loop closes — or stays broken — at the system level, not the symptom level.

### 5. What integrated actually looks like
A protocol that addresses the mucosal barrier (BPC-157 + L-glutamine + aloe), the metabolic terrain (food and movement before pharmacology), the hormonal substrate (only after the inflammatory load is down), and the cognitive endpoint (measured, not assumed). This is what 30 years of practice taught me. The single-symptom approach is the insulting behavior.
