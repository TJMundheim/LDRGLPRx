## 1. What Genesis is and how they sell

- **Company.** Regenerative-medicine company formed in 2023 by NOVO Bio Alliance and DG Medical. HQ: 15500 Roosevelt Blvd, Suite 104, Clearwater, FL 33760. Phone 1-855-320-7559. Patient inquiries: patient-inquiry@genesisregenerative.com. Clinician inquiries: clinician-inquiry@genesisregenerative.com. (GR/about-us/; GR/patient-resources/product-safety/)
- **Three-step "Roadmap to Health".** Assess (PGX/NGX genomic testing), Repair (RPA and MUSE-infused RPA), Maintain (needle-free Push Patch peptides). (GR/roadmap-to-health/; launch release dated Aug 25, 2026 at GR/in-the-news/)
- **Clinician-only model.** Patients are sent to "find a Genesis clinician" rather than ordering. The Find a Clinician page says patients cannot order directly. (GR/about-us/; GR/patient-resources/find-genesis-clinician/)
- **Patient inquiry route.**
  - A free, "private, HIPAA-Compliant" Genesis Lead Intake Form. It asks for name, email, phone, DOB, state, how you heard about Genesis, and Clinician or Patient.
  - After submission, the Patient Liaison Team can set up a personalized consult about clinician selection.
  - There is also a clinician map search (10 to 500 km radius).
  - (GR/patient-resources/find-genesis-clinician/)
- **Referral marketing program.** The clinician page offers "pre-qualified patient referrals" via the HIPAA-compliant Patient Liaison program. It also lists legally-vetted patient education materials, quarterly case-study Zooms, weekly Q&A emails from the Clinical Advisory Board, and one-on-one Medical Director consults. Few details are public. A secure Clinician Resources portal needs a login (https://genesisregenerative.clinic/clinical/login.php, not accessed). (GR/clinician-resources/education-administrative-referral-marketing/)
- **No public prices.** Neither the FAQ, the product pages, nor the T&C list a price. The FAQ says cost "varies by condition and provider" and is not typically covered by insurance. (GR/faq/)
- **Not described as a pharmacy.** The "lab/pharmacy" characterization is not on the site. No pharmacy license, state board registration or 503A/503B status appears on any page I read. The only regulatory documents posted are the Vita Tissue International FDA registration (RPA, tissue) and Resolve Molecular Labs CLIA/CAP certificates (PGX/NGX testing). (GR/patient-resources/product-safety/; GR/about-us/ says no licenses are mentioned)
- **Patient-direct fulfillment is a stated clinician-facing feature.** The Push Patch clinician one-pager says: "Patient direct auto-ship and bill available, eliminating patient travel and staff time, while providing a residual revenue stream to your practice." So Genesis's own model is the clinician's practice gets residual revenue and Genesis ships and bills. That differs from "My4MLife collects payment." (GR/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GENESIS-2026-PUSH-PATCH-ONE-PAGER.pdf)
- **Who owns the product.** Brochures present Push Patch as a product "proudly represented by" Genesis, a "DG Medical / Novo Bio" company (GR/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/GENESIS-2026-PUSH-PATCH-BROCHURE.pdf). The CoAs and the pushpatch.com site show the maker/brand owner is **Push Patch, LLC** (client "Push Patch / PushPatch.com").

## 2. Everything about Push Patch

**Sources:** GR/patient-resources/genesis-products/, GR/clinician-resources/genesis-products/, the one-pagers and brochures linked in section 6, the IFU PDF, and PP.

- **Technology.** "Needle-free" transdermal system using iontophoresis (low-voltage electrodes in the patch). It provides 12-hour time-release delivery "without daily injections or weekly IV treatments." (GR/clinician-resources/genesis-products/)
- **One-pager mechanism.** Two electrodes, one positive and one negative. The molecule sits under an electrode of the same charge and is driven through the skin, and the current temporarily increases skin permeability. It calls the patch "absolutely pain free," "single use," and "similar to using kinesiology tape (KT tape)." (GR/.../GENESIS-2026-PUSH-PATCH-ONE-PAGER.pdf)
- **Wear configurations (important).** "Available in 14-hour configuration for sensitive skin, and 12-hour configuration for use by active / athletic patients." Sold as a "pre-packaged set of 6 patches with all the necessary supplies." (same one-pager)
  - pushpatch.com markets a **14-hour** patch ("up to 14 hours"). (PP/, PP/technology)
- **Configurations (Genesis one-pager of Aug 18, 2026, "-2" version), all 6-patch sets:**
  - NAD+ 1300 mg / GHK-Cu 5 mg
  - Glutathione 500 mg / GHK-Cu 5 mg
  - BPC-157 2000 mcg / NAD+ 250 mg / GHK-Cu 5 mg
  - KPV 10 mg / NAD+ 250 mg / GHK-Cu 5 mg
  - NAD+ 1300 mg / MOTS-c 5 mg / GHK-Cu 5 mg ("NEW")
  - "Enhanced Glow": NAD+ 250 mg / TB-500 2 mg / BPC-157 2000 mcg / GHK-Cu 15 mg ("NEW")
  - "Wolverine": NAD+ 250 mg / TB-500 2 mg / BPC-157 2000 mcg / GHK-Cu 5 mg ("NEW")
  - Source: GR/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GENESIS-2026-PUSH-PATCH-ONE-PAGER-2.pdf. This matches our seven blends.
  - Other Genesis documents disagree (see section 7): the other one-pager and the April brochure.
- **Benefits stated by Genesis.** Needle-free and easy to apply; avoids the GI tract; sustained, time-release delivery "increases the 'Area Under the Curve (AUC)'" versus a bolus of short half-life therapeutics. Tagline: "More Energy. Better Focus. Faster Recovery. Needle Free." No numbers are given for AUC. (one-pagers)
- **Application and prep: the manufacturer's IFU.**
  - Document: "Handling Instructions for PushPatch Kits+", dated April 1, 2024. (GR/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/push-patch-manufacturers-ifu-instructions.pdf; linked from GR/patient-resources/clinical-articles-ifu/ and GR/clinician-resources/journal-articles-ifu/)
  - Skin prep: remove hair, clean with soap and water or an alcohol pad, dry completely.
  - Reconstitute: the kit has an amber vial of lyophilized powder and a blue plastic ampule of sterile water. Add 2 ml of sterile water, cap, shake.
  - Load the pad: press the white pad of the iontophoresis patch against the open vial, invert, and wet the whole pad. Use drops from the ampule for dry spots.
  - Apply: peel the white backing, stick the adhesive edges flat. For high-motion areas, use KT tape. Activate by pulling the clear plastic tab all the way out.
  - Remove: wet with warm soapy water and peel slowly (do not remove dry). Clean the area and apply moisturizer or serum. **Wait at least 24 hours before applying a new patch to the same area.**
  - Patient brochure version (FINAL-GENESIS-PUSH-PATCH-BROCHURE.pdf), 5 steps: prep with an alcohol pad and provided "Skin-Prep", reconstitute the amber vial with **1.5 ml** sterile water, load the pad, apply and pull the "arrow" tab, remove with warm soapy water.
  - **The two documents differ on diluent volume (2 ml vs 1.5 ml).**
- **IFU video.** An mp4 is embedded on the IFU pages: https://genesisregenerative.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/push-patch-ifu-video.mp4. I did not view it.
- **Storage (IFU).** At or below 25 C / 77 F, in original amber vials and foil bags, dry (moisture clumps the lyophilized powder). The wording refers to "the injectable NAD+ kit", so it appears adapted from a generic kit sheet.
- **Contraindications ("Do not use this kit if you have").** Epilepsy or seizures; pacemakers; metal implants near the patch site; pregnancy; recent wounds, skin grafting or scars at the patch site. (IFU PDF above)
- **Other safety text.** Nothing on Genesis pages about adverse reactions, skin irritation, or drug interactions for Push Patch. The FAQ side-effect answer is RPA-only.
- **Weekly cadence.** I found **no** Genesis, pushpatch.com or IFU statement that the patch is "one per week" or "six weeks." Genesis says only "set of 6 patches." (see section 7)
- **Auto-ship.** One-pager: "auto-ship and bill available" (no interval stated). The 6-week auto-ship interval is not on any Genesis page.
- **Manufacturer's site (pushpatch.com).**
  - Describes "High-Dose NAD+ Patches", a "14 hour" iontophoresis patch, and an FAQ that is actually about "bioregulators."
  - Says "Push Patch requires a prescription from your healthcare provider." (PP/) A search-result snippet says it uses "FDA approved iontophoresis patches (prescription required)." The "FDA-Approved Technology" claim was seen in the PP/about page summary and not independently verified.
  - Includes the standard "not evaluated by the FDA" disclaimer.
  - Publishes no PK or AUC data, no citations and no application instructions. (PP/technology, PP/faq, PP/about)
  - Third-party resellers (Liberty MD, Grow with Vitality, Anthro Botanica, Hope Brain Center) show the product sold through clinics and practices (WebSearch results only).

## 5. RPA (Regenerative Protein Array) summary

- **What it is.** "The next generation beyond PRP and Stem Cells"; proteins (cytokines, growth factors, miRNA) derived from placental tissue, all three layers; acellular, no living cells, no DNA transfer; Roadmap page says "320+" verified proteins. (GR/patient-resources/genesis-products/; GR/faq/; GR/roadmap-to-health/)
- **MUSE-Infused RPA.** RPA plus protein from MUSE cells: "0.50 mg of RPA with a 20% MUSE protein enhancement." (GR/clinician-resources/genesis-products/)
- **Indications.** No specific list. "10+ clinical specialties", "60+ healthcare concerns", plus anti-aging. Reference pages group by orthopedic, cognition, cardiac, hair, degenerative disc, etc. (GR/patient-resources/clinical-articles-ifu/). The patient brochures come in Medical and Aesthetics versions (links in section 6).
- **Delivery.** "Most administration methods are minimally invasive"; injection-site swelling or redness can occur and "typically resolv[e] within hours." (GR/faq/). RPA is a clinician-administered injectable. RPA is not the Push Patch and has no iontophoresis connection.
- **Regulatory.** Made in FDA-registered facilities (Vita Tissue International) under human cell and tissue product standards. The FAQ says FDA regulates manufacturing and "allows licensed clinicians to make use and care decisions." It does not claim FDA approval. (GR/faq/ Q6; product-safety page)
- **Price.** Not public. "Not typically covered by insurance"; clinicians often offer financing. (GR/faq/ Q11)

## 7. Contradictions and gaps versus our live page (https://my4mlife.com/go/push-patch)

Highest priority first.

1. **No clearance or prescription step on our page.** The live page is a straight "Buy" with Stripe checkout and never mentions medical clearance, a prescription, or clinician review. The manufacturer says a prescription is required (PP/). Genesis sells only through clinicians and refuses patient orders (GR/patient-resources/find-genesis-clinician/). The page's only "ask a person" path is the care coordinator at /consult.
2. **The 276% / 163% numbers have no public source.** I found no AUC figures on genesisregenerative.com or pushpatch.com. Genesis says only that AUC increases. Our footnote attributes the data to "Push Patch, LLC", so we need the actual data document, the exact dose comparators ("daily 50 mg injections", "500 mg weekly IV"), and written permission to publish. pushpatch.com's own technology page says it has no PK data.
3. **"One patch a week for six weeks" is not found in any Genesis or manufacturer document.** Genesis says "set of 6 patches." Nothing states weekly dosing. Our whole hero ("One patch a week") rests on this. Confirm with Genesis and clinician-of-record.
4. **Wear time and configuration.** Genesis offers 12-hour (active/athletic) and 14-hour (sensitive skin) configurations; the manufacturer markets 14 hours. Our page says 12 hours for everyone with no skin-sensitivity choice. Confirm which ships.
5. **"Prepare the pad" understates what the IFU requires.** Per the IFU the patient reconstitutes a lyophilized powder in an amber vial with sterile water (2 ml in the IFU, 1.5 ml in the brochure), wets the pad, applies and activates a tab, removes only when wet, and must wait 24 hours before reusing a site. Our page says "applicator tube" and "blend vial"; the IFU describes an amber vial, a blue plastic sterile-water ampule, a Skin-Prep, and an alcohol pad. Our "In the box" list (6 patches, 6 vials, 6 applicator tubes) may not match the real kit contents. The "no needles at any step" claim looks fine, but verify there are no needle-type components.
6. **Contraindications missing.** The IFU lists epilepsy or seizures, pacemakers, metal implants near the site, pregnancy, and recent wounds, grafts or scars at the site. Our FAQ only says "do not apply to broken or irritated skin." These belong in our intake screen and on the page.
7. **Application site.** Our "clean skin of the upper arm or shoulder" is not in Genesis documents ("specific areas of the body"). Our "light tingle" description is also not in Genesis documents (Genesis says "absolutely pain free").
8. **Storage is not mentioned on our page.** IFU: room temperature or below, original amber vials and foil bags, dry.
9. **Formula discrepancies inside Genesis's own materials.**
   - Enhanced Glow: GHK-Cu 15 mg and TB-500 2 mg / BPC-157 2000 mcg (Aug one-pager "-2"; matches our page) versus TB-500 10 mg / BPC-157 2 mg / GHK-Cu 5 mg (April 2026 brochure, which lists only five blends and has no MOTS-c or Wolverine).
   - The other Aug one-pager shows KPV twice and omits MOTS-c.
   - Our Metabolic (MOTS-c) blend appears only in one-pager "-2"; the April brochure does not list it.
   - Get a signed current formulary from Genesis before shipping.
10. **Third-party testing wording.** Our page says every active is third-party tested with HPLC purity and mass-spec identity CoAs "available on request." Genesis already publishes seven CoAs, and the one NAD+ certificate is one lot (KGC31324, tested Jan 2025) at 96.67% purity. The MOTS-c CoA is not named in our sentence, and the TB-500, KPV and MOTS-c CoAs are dated 2026. Link the public CoAs and confirm lots match what ships.
11. **Name and attribution.** We say "Genesis Push Patch". Genesis markets it as "Push Patch" (TM), "proudly represented by" Genesis, made by Push Patch, LLC. Our page never names Genesis or Push Patch, LLC except in the data footnote.
12. **Business-model mismatch to resolve with Genesis.** Genesis's offer is clinician-ordered with Genesis ship and bill and a residual to the practice. Our plan (My4MLife collects payment, medically clears, Genesis ships) is not described anywhere on Genesis's site and needs a written agreement.
13. **No refunds.** "All sales are final" is our policy; Genesis has no public policy to contradict it.
14. **No testimonials on our page and none available.** Genesis's Push Patch video slots are empty.
15. **A copy error on Genesis's patient site** (heading "Push Patch Peptides" above RPA text on GR/patient-resources/genesis-products/). Not our problem, but do not use that page as a source of Push Patch claims.
