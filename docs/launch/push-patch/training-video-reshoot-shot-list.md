# Push Patch video: reshoot shot list (hand shots only)

The animated parts stay as they are. These 11 shots replace the hand clips. Each shot is about 6–10 seconds; I use 2–4 seconds of each, sometimes from two points in the same shot. Do 2 takes of each and keep the best.

## Setup (10 minutes, once)
- **Camera:** iPhone on a tripod or a clamp/overhead mount. Use the main (1×) lens and **shoot horizontal (landscape), 4K, 30 fps.** Horizontal lets me crop square for both the wide and vertical versions.
- **Lock focus and exposure.** Before each shot, tap and hold on the item until "AE/AF LOCK" appears. This stops the focus hunting from the first video.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window to the side, or two lamps. No overhead kitchen light (it causes harsh shadows), and no flash.
- **Surface:** a plain matte surface, not the granite. Best is a dark navy or charcoal placemat, cloth or poster board (it matches the video). Light gray works too.
- **You:** clean hands, trimmed nails, no watch or bracelet.
  - **Table shots:** a plain dark long-sleeve shirt with no pattern.
  - **Arm shots:** a plain short-sleeve shirt with no lettering or logos. The NASA shirt showed up last time.
- **Background behind the arm:** a plain wall or door, nothing busy.
- **Sound:** not needed. The video is muted, so talk freely.
- **Hold still** for about 2 seconds at the start and end of each shot.

## The 11 shots

| # | Shot | Framing | What to do | Length |
|---|------|---------|------------|--------|
| 1 | **Opening the kit** | Overhead or 45°, hands and pouch centered | Open the pouch and slide out the patch, vial and dropper. Lay them out neatly. | 8–10 s |
| 2 | **Full vial** | Close-up, vial filling most of the frame | Hold the amber vial up, label toward the camera, and turn it slowly so the powder shows. | 6 s |
| 3 | **Marked dropper** | Tight close-up on the dropper against the plain surface | Show the two lines you marked with a Sharpie: **full line** and **2 mL line**. Then slowly squeeze from full down to the 2 mL line into the open vial. | 10 s |
| 4 | **Mixing** | Close-up on the vial in your hand | Cap it and tip it gently back and forth, 4–5 times. No shaking. | 6–8 s |
| 5 | **Drip onto the pad** | Overhead, patch flat, white pad centered | Slowly drip the blend onto the **center** of the pad until it's evenly wet (blue). Keep it all in the middle. | 15–20 s (it's slow, that's fine) |
| 6 | **Alcohol pad** | Back of the upper arm, side view | Wipe the area with the alcohol pad. | 5 s |
| 7 | **Roller** | Same arm, slightly closer | Roll gently over an area just bigger than the pad, several passes. | 8 s |
| 8 | **Mild redness** | Same arm, still | Just hold on the area so the mild redness shows clearly. | 4 s |
| 9 | **Skin-prep wipe** | Same arm | Wipe the area with the skin-prep wipe. | 5 s |
| 10 | **Peel the backing** | Overhead, patch on the surface | Peel the backing so the adhesive and wet pad show, holding the little end tab. | 8–10 s |
| 11 | **Apply, seal, pull tab** | Back of the upper arm, side view | Place the pad over the prepped spot, press the edges all the way around, then **pull the clear tab all the way out**. Finish with a 2-second hold on the sealed patch. | 25–30 s |

Shots 6–9 and 11 are on the arm. Having someone else hold the phone is easiest, or use the tripod with the arm turned to the camera. Keep your face out of frame.
