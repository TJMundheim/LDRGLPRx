## TJ's answers (2026-11-16)
- **Q1 Kit:** amber blend vial, a sterile water dropper (about 3.5 mL), the patch, an alcohol pad, a skin-prep wipe, a microneedling roller. Confirmed.
- **Q2 Water:** 1.5 mL. The water dropper holds about 3.5 mL. Mark the full line and the 2 mL line with a marker, then squeeze from the full line down to the 2 mL line, which puts 1.5 mL in the vial. Not all the water is used.
- **Q3 Mixing:** gently, no shaking. The website's "shake to mix" line is updated to match.
- **Q4 Loading:** drip slowly onto the center of the pad.
- **Q5 Site:** described generally ("clean, flat, hair-free, not over a joint"), plus a tip: for most people the back of the upper arm, below the shoulder and above the elbow, is easiest to do yourself. It has minimal hair and doesn't cross a joint, so movement won't loosen the patch.
- **Q6 Roller:** gentle passes; mild redness is normal; don't roll over moles, cuts or irritated skin.
- **Voice:** Ruth (Claude's pick, at TJ's request).
- **Clips:** TJ's hands from the phone video, approved. No face.
