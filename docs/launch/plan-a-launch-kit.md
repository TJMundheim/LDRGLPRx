_Drafted 2026-07-22. Companion to docs/plan/speaking-circuit-traffic-strategy.md and docs/launch/speaking/. This is the "start collecting dollars this week" packet: warm-list outreach, the list broadcast, a launch post, a send order, and a tracker._

## The math (why this hits $2–3K)

~10–14 approved Rx starts at a ~$220 month-1 average = **$2,200–3,080**, plus ~$300–500 back-of-room book cash. Ticket sizes that transact today:
- **GLP-1 weight program** — $199 / $249 / $299 per month (recurring)
- **TRT / ED** — $249 first month, then $129/month
- **Leaky-gut Rx** (compounded Biome NS Rx) — free consult, product margin after
- **Peptides / regenerative** — free consult, higher ticket

Every real-margin dollar flows through: questionnaire → card on file → physician approves → charged. Traffic isn't the constraint; **consult→approval throughput is.** Don't out-market what the provider can actually process this month.

## Rules baked into all copy below

- Credential: **"Dr. TJ Mundheim, NBCE-certified since 1994."** Never "MD." Never the verb "treat."
- Honest tone, no outcome hype. "Physician-supervised." "Card saved, not charged until the physician approves."
- Sender for all outbound: **drtj@my4mlife.com** (Gmail). Warm pieces go **1:1 / individually** — never a mass BCC of former patients.
- Links carry a tracking slug: `?src=warm-list`, `?src=newsletter-launch`, `?src=launch-post`.
