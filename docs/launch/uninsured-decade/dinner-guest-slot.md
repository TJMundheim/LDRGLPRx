# Dinner Guest-Slot Script — "The Uninsured Decade" (Channel 0, 8 minutes)

## Standard version (host does not sell LTC/annuity products)

**[0:00–1:15] Open on Bill and Cindy.**

> "Before [Host] wraps up tonight, I want to take eight minutes and put one number in front of you that most retirement plans never price in.
>
> Picture a couple — call them Bill and Cindy. Seventy years old, retired, active, doing everything right. Here's the number: for a couple like that, the odds that one of them develops dementia are about two in three — that's a 2025 Nature Medicine study, not a guess. And then one of them is diagnosed."

**[1:15–2:15] The $0 Medicare line.**

> "Here's the part almost nobody in this room knows going in: Medicare pays zero dollars toward custodial care. That's not a gap in the plan — it's Medicare's own written rule. If custodial care is the only care you need, Medicare doesn't cover it. Full stop."

**[2:15–3:30] The collapsed LTC market.**

> "So the natural next thought is, 'fine, that's what long-term-care insurance is for.' Except the market for that has largely collapsed. Twenty-five years ago there were over a hundred carriers selling standalone LTC policies. Today, fewer than a dozen still write new ones — and the ones still in the game have raised rates on existing policyholders by sixty, eighty, sometimes over a hundred percent. For a lot of people in this room, by the time you're motivated to buy a policy, you may not qualify, or the premium is a second mortgage."

**[3:30–4:45] "You can't insure it. You can only underwrite it."**

> "So here's the reframe I want to leave you with tonight: you can't insure this risk anymore. You can only underwrite it — the same way an underwriter lowers your premium by lowering your risk profile."

**[4:45–5:15] 30-second what-underwriting-looks-like.**

> "In practice that means three things: know your own numbers — net worth against your state's actual cost of care, so there's no surprise. Get the paperwork done now, while everyone involved can still weigh in — powers of attorney, health-care directives. And work the modifiable factors — sleep, movement, metabolic health — that the research links to a meaningfully lower personal risk. I want to be careful here: nothing prevents dementia, and nobody honest will tell you otherwise. But the evidence says you can shift your own odds. That's underwriting."

**[5:15–6:00] Book on the seat + QR.**

> "There's a short book on your seat tonight — it walks through this whole picture in about forty-five minutes of reading, no cost, nothing to buy. And if you want your own number — a free, seven-minute look at where you personally stand — there's a QR code on the screen. That's the only thing I'm asking you to do tonight."

**[6:00–8:00] Hand back to host.**

> "That's it from me — I'm not selling anything up here. [Host], back to you, and thank you for having me."

## Variant — host sells annuities or LTC hybrid products

Use when the host's practice actively sells annuities, hybrid life/LTC, or similar products. Do not step on their close.

- **Cut the "collapsed LTC market" beat to one sentence**, and hand the "what to do about it" ground explicitly to the host: replace the 30-second underwriting beat with — *"The good news is [Host] and their team spend every day helping families structure exactly this kind of risk — that's their job, not mine. My piece of it is the other side: the modifiable-risk-factor work that can lower your personal odds, which no policy can do for you."*
- **Do not use the phrase "you can't insure it"** in this variant — it can read as undercutting the host's product. Replace with: *"A policy can help you fund the risk. It can't lower the risk itself — that part is still on you."*
- Everything else (open, $0 Medicare line, book + QR close) stays the same.

## Three likely audience questions — 20-second answers

1. **"Doesn't my [annuity/LTC hybrid] already cover this?"**
   → "It may cover a real piece of the funding side — that's a great question for [Host] tonight. What it can't do is lower the underlying risk. That's the part I focus on, and it's free to start tonight."

2. **"Is this a sales pitch?"**
   → "No — nothing I've said is for sale from up here. The QR code is a free seven-minute assessment, that's the only ask. The book's already on your seat, no charge."

3. **"How much of this can I actually prevent?"**
   → "Honestly — none of it is preventable with certainty, and I'd be lying if I said otherwise. What the research supports is shifting your odds through modifiable factors. That's expected-value thinking, not a guarantee, but it's the only lever left once insurance stops being reliable."
