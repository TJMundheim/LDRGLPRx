# Why estate attorneys & financial planners would promote The Uninsured Decade (2026-09-19)

Source: TJ + Fable session 2026-09-19. Standing rule: endorse the professional partner; never a competing solution.

## Estate attorneys
1. Capacity preservation — every will/trust/POA depends on testamentary capacity; decline = contested/void documents.
2. Solves their hardest conversation — the book raises incapacity so they don't have to.
3. The uninsured gap is their cleanup job (Medicaid 5-yr lookback, no LTC insurance, $0 Medicare custodial).
4. Re-engagement — plans are one-time; book/dinner/annual cognitive check-in create touchpoints → amendments, referrals.
5. Differentiation + CE credit at estate-planning councils.

## Financial planners (stronger buyer)
1. **AUM retention** — memory care ~$8–15K/mo private pay for years comes out of the portfolio; averted decline = assets stay invested, fees continue.
2. Their retirement plan assumes a healthy client — decline is the unmodeled risk.
3. Regulatory duty (FINRA 2165 trusted contact / diminished-capacity monitoring) — the book is the graceful way to have a mandated conversation.
4. Spouse/heirs are the next clients — get ahead of decline = keep the generational relationship.
5. They already buy seminar dinners; a clinician on the uninsured decade draws better than an annuity pitch.
6. Client gift that says "your mind, not just your money" — bulk copies ≈ $4.

## What both need
- Endorse-the-partner posture; zero medical advice/liability; turnkey kit (8-min deck, co-branded insert, client letter, referral path to coordinator call). <1 hour to deploy.
