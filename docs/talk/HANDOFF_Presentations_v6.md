**How the decks are built:** each deck is generated by a Node script using pptxgenjs (16:9 wide, 13.333 × 7.5 in), followed by the pptx skill's apply_theme.js, validate.py and a LibreOffice render for visual QA. Edit build.js and rebuild; don't hand-edit the PPTX. Fonts are Cambria for headings and Calibri for body text. Speaker notes are set per slide with addNotes. Presenter scripts are made with reportlab: the slides are rendered to JPG, then each page shows one slide image with "What to say" underneath.

**Shared design:** dark navy (#0A1430 / #0B1530) with gold (#D4A94E / #E2B45C), plus generated "constellation network" backgrounds with a gold wave line (bg.py / bg2.py, made with PIL). Icons are react-icons (Font Awesome) in gold-ringed circles, on semi-transparent rounded cards with thin borders. No accent bars, no underlines under titles.

**Genesis v6 design changes (Nov 20, 2026):** purple and green were removed; the palette is navy and gold with one supporting blue (#7FB0FF / #A9C4F5). Every icon is a gold icon in a navy circle with a gold ring. The highlighted card (RPA, Repair, 320+) is gold-filled with navy text; callout cards have a gold outline. ## PROJECT 2: My4MLife talk, "Begin with the End in Mind: Body, Brain, Bank" (v4)

**Purpose:** an entertaining, educational 30–40 minute talk for almost any crowd. It promotes Dr. TJ's three books (all on Amazon) and my4mlife.com. The strategy is a Trojan horse: the books, education and entertainment earn trust and assessments, and the real revenue (peptides, the Push Patch, GLP-1s) comes later through the free assessment → care-coordinator call → physician-reviewed plan. **No products are sold from the stage.**

**The books:**
- **Count Yourself Skinny** (Body): never count calories; count to 32 chews per bite. Six counts: 32 chews, 2,500 steps (the floor), 12→16 hours between dinner and breakfast (3 hours before bed), 10 minutes of morning light, 2 glasses of water before noon, 7 of 7 nights of 7+ hours. "One walk, five boxes." "Never miss twice." Iowa State study: 50% more chews → ~10% less eaten; doubled chews → ~15% less.
- **Begin with the End in Mind** (Brain): "Don't lose your identity and your dignity while you still have a choice." Alzheimer's starts in midlife (changes can begin 20+ years before symptoms). Up to 45% of dementia may be preventable or delayed (Lancet 2024, 14 risk factors). The 4M System: Mind (destination), Muscle (engine), Mitigate (remove harm), Motivate (make it stick).
- **The Uninsured Decade** (Bank): Medicare pays $0 toward custodial care. Ten-year staged bill: ~$35–40K, ~$170–180K, ~$290–340K, for a total of ~$495–560K. $405,262 average lifetime cost with 70% borne by family; 63% higher mortality for strained spousal caregivers. "The premium you can still pay": ~10 hours a month.
