## Part 2: Money, rights, and exits

### Money: flat, and only from the book

| Term | Draft position |
|---|---|
| **Option A (recommended)** | **No royalty to Co-Author.** Co-Author buys copies at Publisher's print cost plus shipping, and keeps the audience and credit |
| **Option B (fallback)** | Flat dollar amount per copy sold at retail, after stated editing costs are recouped, set in proportion to pages written. **Zero on any copy that Co-Author, their firm, or a related party buys, gives away, or causes to be bought in bulk** |
| **Option C** | 50/50 only for a national name who brings a publisher. Separate negotiation |
| **Health-side money** | **None, in either direction, ever.** No fee, credit, revenue share, discount, gift, free service, or other benefit tied to or varying with the assessment, consults, labs, products, or the Captain's Table. Applies to Co-Author, their firm, staff, and related parties. Breach is grounds for immediate termination |
| **No tracking for pay** | Any `?src=` link for Co-Author's readers reports aggregate counts only, with small groups suppressed, and is never used to compute any payment |
| **Client data** | Co-Author never receives names, scores, bookings, or any health information |
| **Editing cost** | Publisher pays developmental and copy editing of the core. Co-Author pays their firm's compliance review and any help writing their chapters |
