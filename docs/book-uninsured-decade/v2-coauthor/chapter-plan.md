# Second edition: chapter plan with one co-author

**Is doing more now a rabbit hole? Yes.** No planner has been contacted, v1 is not in print, Marla has not signed off, and no attorney has read a word of this packet. Sample chapters, an Advisor Edition template, or more polish on these files would be work on a deal that may not exist, and a planner's real answer will come from a compliance department you cannot predict from your desk. This folder is enough to start a conversation, so stop here. **The single next action:** when Marla returns the foreword, ask her two things in one sitting: sign the one-page permission for later editions, and name the two planners she trusts with her own clients. Everything else in this folder waits until v1 is live and the one hour of counsel is done.

**Status: DRAFT for TJ.** Internal planning document, not for a candidate. Word counts for v1 were counted from `draft/_MASTER.md` on 2026-11-04, before any patches. Foreword counted separately. v2 targets are my estimates, to be negotiated with the co-author.

**The three rules:** the planner owns the money. TJ owns the health. Marla owns the law. No one signs for anyone else's pages, and v2 says so in print.

## What this plan does not include

- It does not put the co-author's chapters in the Advisor Edition. No planner hands clients a rival's name, so the two stay separate products.
- It does not promise any planner that the protocol keeps assets invested. No evidence supports that. See the pitch.
- It does not ask anyone to write a chapter on spec.

## Order of events, if the first calls go well

1. v1 live, with Marla's permission letter in hand.

3. Hand-verify the candidate. Send the note.
4. They read v1. Twenty-minute call.
5. Compliance question first: will their firm permit it? If no, stop or go to the journalist fallback.
6. Term sheet through both attorneys. **No chapter gets written before it is signed.** That is the Rich Dad lesson.
