Which policy in your folder pays for memory care?

You spent thirty years building coverage so that nothing could land on your family as a bill. Then read the fine print on this line. Medicare doesn't cover custodial care if it's the only care you need. Where a policy or hybrid rider fits your plan, it funds part of the risk. No contract lowers the odds.

This is a book about money before it is a book about health, and it is written to whoever holds the pen.

Inside
  • A ten-year ledger for one household, priced line by line, with sources and dates
  • What Medicare, Medicaid, and a thinned-out insurance market leave on the table, and why your advisor's review matters more now
  • The odds stated as odds, including the math that does not break even
  • A 30-day plan that ends with an hour across the table from your advisor, attorney, or CPA

Nothing here prevents, treats, cures, or reverses dementia. The work may lower the odds. Your professionals fund and structure the risk. You need both.
