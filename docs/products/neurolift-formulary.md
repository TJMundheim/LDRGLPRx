## TL;DR — What this product is

**NeuroLift** is a once-daily powdered supplement combining **creatine monohydrate**, an **NO2-boosting blend** (L-citrulline malate + standardized beetroot extract), and a **functional electrolyte matrix**. Single product, dual mission:

1. **Cognitive** — published research shows creatine improves working memory, intelligence test performance, and resilience to sleep-deprived cognitive decline. NO2 boosters increase cerebral blood flow. This is the underrated brain-health story most men have never heard.
2. **Body composition / sarcopenia mitigation** — creatine + resistance training is the single most-evidenced behavioral combination for preserving lean muscle mass with age. Lean muscle is metabolically active tissue that drives glucose disposal, reduces visceral fat, and lowers the inflammatory cytokine load that crosses the blood-brain barrier.

The product is on-thesis for My4MLife because it operates on **both ends of the 4M framework simultaneously** — Muscle (sarcopenia prevention, metabolic engine) and Mind (cognitive function). Few supplements do.

## Research basis — the dual-pillar story

### Cognitive benefits (Mind pillar)

- **Rae et al., 2003 (Proc Royal Soc B):** 5g/day creatine for 6 weeks significantly improved working memory and intelligence test performance (Raven's Advanced Progressive Matrices) in vegetarian adults. Baseline brain creatine matters — vegetarians had biggest gains, but improvements were replicated in omnivores in subsequent studies.
- **Avgerinos et al., 2018 (Experimental Gerontology):** Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 RCTs concluded creatine supplementation improves **short-term memory and intelligence/reasoning in healthy individuals**, with larger effect sizes in older adults and vegetarians.
- **Gordji-Nejad et al., 2024 (Scientific Reports):** Single high-dose creatine (0.35 g/kg, ~25g for 70kg adult) improved cognitive performance and reduced fatigue during sleep deprivation within ~3 hours. Confirms acute cognitive utility — not just chronic.
- **Mechanism:** Brain phosphocreatine acts as an ATP buffer. Under cognitive stress, sleep deprivation, or high mental demand, phosphocreatine availability becomes rate-limiting for sustained neural function. Creatine supplementation increases brain phosphocreatine stores by ~5-15%, providing measurable cognitive headroom.

### Sarcopenia / muscle / metabolic benefits (Muscle pillar)

- **ISSN Position Stand on Creatine, Kreider et al., 2017 / updated 2021 (JISSN):** Creatine monohydrate is the single most-evidenced ergogenic supplement. Safe long-term. 3-5g/day chronic dosing is sufficient; loading phase optional.
- **Chilibeck et al., 2017 (Open Access J Sports Med):** Meta-analysis: creatine + resistance training in older adults increases lean mass by ~1.4 kg and strength gains by ~10-15% vs resistance training alone. Direct sarcopenia mitigation.
- **Forbes et al., 2022 (Nutrients):** Creatine + exercise reduces fat mass and improves body composition; effects on visceral fat specifically mediated through increased lean mass + improved insulin sensitivity.
- **Mechanism:** Increased intramuscular phosphocreatine supports higher training volume → greater stimulus for hypertrophy → preserved/increased lean mass → improved metabolic flexibility → reduced visceral adiposity → reduced systemic inflammatory cytokine load.

### NO2-boosting blend rationale

- **L-citrulline:** Bypasses hepatic first-pass metabolism that limits L-arginine bioavailability. Multiple RCTs show 6-8g/day improves endothelial function, exercise performance, and blood pressure (Bailey et al., Pérez-Guisado et al.).
- **Dietary nitrates (beetroot):** Reduced to nitrite by oral commensal bacteria, then to NO. Lowers resting BP (~3-5 mmHg systolic). **Wightman et al., 2015 (Physiol Behav):** Acute beetroot juice (containing ~430mg nitrate) increased cerebral blood flow during cognitive tasks and improved performance on demanding mental arithmetic.
- **The cognitive-vascular link:** Cerebral blood flow declines ~0.4-0.5% per year in adulthood. Improving endothelial function and NO availability is one of the few directly-actionable levers for maintaining cerebral perfusion across decades.

### Electrolyte rationale

- Most men in My4MLife's demographic are sodium-conservative (post-1990s low-salt era), often clinically dehydrated, and frequently magnesium-deficient. The electrolyte matrix isn't filler — it directly enables the performance and recovery the creatine + NO blend is designed to deliver.

## Contraindications + label warnings (must appear on product packaging)

- **Renal impairment:** Creatine increases muscle creatine stores and can elevate serum creatinine (a kidney function marker) without indicating actual kidney damage. Men with diagnosed renal insufficiency should consult a physician before use.
- **Blood pressure medications:** Beetroot extract and L-citrulline both lower BP. Men on antihypertensive medication should monitor BP carefully when adding NeuroLift.
- **PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil/tadalafil):** Combined NO-pathway stimulation theoretically additive; clinical relevance unclear but warrants physician guidance.
- **Pregnancy / lactation:** Not studied; not recommended.
- **General disclaimer:** Standard FDA dietary supplement disclaimer — "These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."

## Open questions for clinical advisory board

1. **Loading phase:** Standard creatine loading (20g/day x 5-7 days) accelerates saturation but isn't necessary. Should the product label suggest loading for first week, or just steady-state 5g/day?
2. **Pycnogenol inclusion:** Strong evidence base but doubles ingredient cost. Include in base product or reserve for premium variant?
3. **Magnesium form:** Glycinate (GI-tolerant, well-studied) vs L-threonate (premium, superior CNS penetration, but ~2x cost and less long-term data). Glycinate recommended for base; threonate possibly for premium "NeuroLift Plus" variant.
4. **Acute cognitive dose:** Given Gordji-Nejad 2024 showing 25g acute dose improves cognition under sleep deprivation, should we offer a "demanding day" 2-scoop protocol option (10g creatine)? Marketing risk: makes the standard dose feel like a teaser.
5. **Pre-workout vs morning vs evening dosing:** Creatine timing isn't critical for chronic benefit, but NO boosters peak at 60-90 min post-ingestion. Recommended use case: ~60 min before mentally or physically demanding activity (morning routine, training, deep work block, important meeting).
