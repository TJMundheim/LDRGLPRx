# RPA (Regenerative Protein Array) — source notes for book v20 (2026-09-02)
Source: genesisregenerative.com (brand NEVER named in print — book says "regenerative protein array" generically).
Compliance: "help"/"support" not "treat/cure"; clinician-administered; educational.

## What it is (from the Clinical Overview brochure, 2026-04)
- Array of proteins, growth factors, cytokines (+ miRNA per product page) derived from PLACENTAL tissue.
- Made by placing placental tissue in a specialized bioreactor that stimulates it to produce a rich array of
  cytokines/growth factors → concentrated "regenerative messages" of placental tissue.
- Thesis: therapeutic power of regenerative medicine lies NOT in injected stem cells turning into target tissue,
  but in modifying the surrounding cellular environment via key biological messages (paracrine signaling).
- Potency: "estimated an order of magnitude more potent than PRP."
- Mechanism: "reprogramming" the local environment of damaged tissue; reactivates transcription/translation;
  restores tissue balance; "reintroduces the regenerative messages the body loses with age."
- Safety: acellular, DNA-free → lower risk of adverse reactions/off-target effects vs cell-based therapies.
- Delivery: local (at site) and/or systemic; often combined. Routes (per TJ memory): intra-articular, IV,
  nebulizer, intrathecal, intranasal — covers nearly any tissue incl. CNS.
- Clinical scope claim: 10+ specialties, 60+ healthcare concerns (not enumerated publicly).
- Indications TJ names: joint injuries, post-concussion syndrome, dementia/cognitive deficiency.

## MUSE-infused RPA (brochure 2026-08)
- RPA + proteins extracted from MUSE cells (Multilineage-differentiating Stress-Enduring; Tohoku University
  discovery; stress-resistant, highly pluripotent, non-tumorigenic). Acellular/non-DNA. 0.50 mg RPA + 20% MUSE
  protein enhancement.
- MUSE biology: cellular resilience, anti-inflammatory signaling, cytoprotection, angiogenic support,
  "neuro-supportive repair signaling", repair-priority traits.
- Refs: Promising Role of MUSE Cells (ScienceDirect 2025); MUSE: New Era (Cells 2023, mdpi 12/13/1676);
  Safety & Clinical Effects of MUSE product (PMC10686030); Powerful Tool for Tissue Damage Repair (PMC11169632);
  Direct Contribution of MUSE to Tissue Repair (Cell Transplant); Secretome of MUSE cells (Cell Cycle 2016).

## Cognition / neuroinflammation literature cited on site
- Umbilical MSC-derived exosomal miR-146a-5p reduces microglial neuroinflammation via IRAK1/TRAF6 after
  ischemic stroke (Aging; aging-us.com/article/202466).
- Regulatory microRNAs and vascular cognitive impairment and dementia (CNS Neurosci Ther; 10.1111/cns.13472).
- A microRNA signature that correlates with cognition and is a target against cognitive decline
  (EMBO Mol Med; 10.15252/emmm.202013659).
- MicroRNA: implications for regeneration and repair of neurodegenerative diseases (Semin Cell Dev Biol).
