## Revenue Model Notes

### Affiliate vs in-house vs white-label

For Phase 1 (next 6 months), most products should launch as **affiliate links to vetted brands** to avoid inventory risk:
- Air filtration (Air Doctor, IQAir, Coway, Awair)
- Water filtration (AquaTru, Berkey, Waterdrop)
- Red light (Joovv, Mito Red, PlatinumLED)
- Grounding (Earthing.com, Hooga)
- EMF (Shieldex, Yshield, TriField)

Affiliate margin is 8–15% typical. On a $400 air purifier sale, that's $32–$60 to My4MLife with no inventory, no fulfillment, no returns.

For Phase 2 (post-PMF, MRR > $50K), **white-label the high-volume gateway SKUs** in-house:
- Night-light packs
- Blackout dots
- Faraday pouches
- Mineral drops
- Grounding patches

These are low-COGS, high-margin (~70% gross), perfect for in-house branding without the operational lift of complex electronics or filtration systems.

### Subscription / recurring revenue

The big LTV multipliers come from consumables:
- HVAC filter subscriptions (every 90 days)
- Water filter cartridge subscriptions (every 6 months)
- Mineral drop subscriptions (monthly)
- Grounding patches (monthly resupply)
- Red-light bulb refreshes (annual)

A typical Insider household running an air filter + water filter + RO mineral drops generates an estimated **$400–$700 per year in recurring filter/consumable revenue alone** — before any one-time durable-goods purchase.

### Bundle pricing

Three bundle tiers per environmental sub-category:
- **Starter** (~$99) — gateway, low-commitment, drives adoption
- **Mid-tier** (~$249) — covers the highest-impact interventions in that sub-category
- **Sanctuary** (~$799+) — premium, Insider Concierge level, completes the environment

Member-only pricing: Insider tier gets 10% off everything; Insider Plus 15%; Insider Concierge 20% + free shipping. Drives upgrade conversion.

### Cross-category bundling

The biggest revenue play is the **"4M Sleep Sanctuary"** — combines key SKUs from all 5 sub-categories into a single optimized bedroom protocol:

- Light: 4 circadian bulbs + 1 night-light + 1 pack blackout dots + 1 pair red-lens blue-blockers
- Air: bedroom HEPA + carbon air purifier
- Water: shower head filter (since you shower before bed)
- EMF: faraday phone pouch + EMF meter
- Grounding: full grounding sheet set

Targeted price ~$899 (vs ~$1,200 retail individually). Insider members get 15% off — paid into the program in 3 months at typical Insider pricing. Single-strongest "I'm serious about this" purchase signal. Highest-LTV cohort.
