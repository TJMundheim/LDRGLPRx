## MitoVita — creatine + L-citrulline + beetroot + electrolytes

### Touches all 4 Ms

- **Mind** — Brain phosphocreatine improves working memory and intelligence test performance; the NO blend improves cerebral blood flow during demanding cognitive work
- **Muscle** — The most-published ingredient for slowing age-related muscle loss; +1.4 kg additional lean mass in older men in peer-reviewed meta-analysis
- **Mitigate** — Healthy vascular function (the same upstream mechanism that supports cognitive longevity and sexual performance); preserved lean muscle drives insulin sensitivity and visceral fat reduction
- **Motivate** — More energy under load. More physical, cognitive, and sexual performance available when you need it

### Why you'll want this for life

> *Your body's ability to make creatine declines as you age. Endogenous production drops, dietary intake from meat alone doesn't replace it at the doses your muscle and brain need. Vasodilation declines too — endothelial function falls about 0.4–0.5% per year in adulthood without active maintenance. Stopping MitoVita at 50 means accepting accelerated decline in three systems (cognitive, muscle, vascular) that don't repair themselves.*

## NeuroBridge — methylated B-complex

### Touches all 4 Ms

- **Mind** — Bioavailable B-vitamins your brain actually uses to make serotonin, dopamine, and GABA — the chemicals behind mood, focus, and calm
- **Muscle** — B-vitamins convert food into ATP — the energy your muscles run on. Without them, the protein you eat doesn't make it to the cells that need it
- **Mitigate** — Clears homocysteine, a top independent risk factor for heart disease and dementia. Methylated forms bypass the genetic bottleneck (MTHFR variants) that limits up to 60% of the population
- **Motivate** — Steady energy. Freedom from low-grade fatigue. Lifts the brain fog men accept as "just getting older"

### Why you'll want this for life

> *Up to 60% of people carry genetic variants (MTHFR) that block the standard B-vitamin conversion process. That doesn't change with age — it's a permanent feature of your biology. Common medications make it worse: PPIs (acid blockers) deplete B12, metformin depletes B12, alcohol depletes folate. Modern diet replaces what's lost only at the slowest possible rate. NeuroBridge bypasses the genetic bottleneck and replaces what your environment is taking. The men who don't take a methylated B-complex past 40 are running on a chronic depletion that gets worse, not better.*

The men who don't fix sleep are accepting accelerated cognitive aging, slower recovery, and weaker testosterone — every single night. SleepRestore is the cheapest, simplest, highest-leverage intervention in the entire protocol.*

### Touches all 4 Ms

- **Mind** — Vitamin D is a steroid hormone with receptors throughout the brain. Astaxanthin is one of the few antioxidants powerful enough to cross the blood-brain barrier
- **Muscle** — D3 and Boron support testosterone — the upstream hormone behind muscle preservation and recovery
- **Mitigate** — K2 routes calcium to bones and away from arteries (calcium in arteries is what causes most heart disease). D3 modulates immune function and inflammation across every system

### Why you'll want this for life

> *Your skin's ability to make vitamin D from sunlight drops about 50% by age 70. Modern indoor life cuts effective sun exposure by another 80%. Most men over 40 are clinically deficient and don't know it. Testosterone declines about 1% per year after 30 — without active support, the line goes one direction only. ArmorVita doesn't just fix a deficiency — it maintains a system that gets harder to maintain every year. Stop at 50 and the system you built collapses faster than it took to build.*
