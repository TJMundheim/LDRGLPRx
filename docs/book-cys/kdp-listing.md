## Description (HTML KDP accepts)

<p><b>You'll never count a calorie. You'll count to 32 instead.</b></p>
<p>The diet industry got fat. This book got skinny.</p>
<p>Count Yourself Skinny takes everything that makes people quit a diet (the math, the scale, the shopping list, the forbidden foods) and strips it down to six easy numbers you can count on your fingers:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Chews</b> per bite</li>
<li><b>Hours</b> between meals</li>
<li><b>Steps</b> per day</li>
<li><b>Minutes</b> of morning light</li>
<li><b>Glasses</b> of water before noon</li>
<li><b>Nights</b> of real sleep</li>
</ul>
<p>Each count gets one short chapter: what to do, why it works, and what to stop doing. Then the morning walk ties five of them into one twenty-five minute habit, and a streak rule keeps you from quitting on a bad day.</p>
<p>A 12-week Count Log is built right into the book, with a page for every week and room for your daily notes. Fill it in with a pen. Every page you complete is proof you showed up.</p>
<p>No food scale. No forbidden foods. Nothing to buy. If you can count to 32, you can do this.</p>
<p><i>Count Yourself Skinny is educational and is not a substitute for care from your own clinician. Talk with your clinician before changing when you eat, especially if you take medication, have diabetes, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating.</i></p>
