Why gut repair is brain repair

Your gut and your brain are one system running on one supply line. When the gut lining is damaged, the brain pays the bill.

  • About 90% of your serotonin — the neurotransmitter behind mood and calm focus — is produced in the gut, not the brain.
  • Roughly half of your dopamine — drive, motivation, reward — is synthesized there too.
  • The vagus nerve is a two-way highway between gut and brain. An inflamed gut sends inflammatory traffic upstream, all day, every day.
  • The damage cascade: processed food, seed oils, alcohol, stress, and medications thin the mucosal barrier → the tight junctions between intestinal cells loosen ("leaky gut") → particles cross into the bloodstream that never should → the immune system reacts → chronic low-grade inflammation reaches the brain as fog, fatigue, low mood, and — over years — accelerated cognitive decline.

Why probiotics alone haven't fixed it

Most people reach for a probiotic and feel nothing. Seeding new bacteria into a damaged, inflamed lining is planting seeds in scorched soil. The lining has to be repaired first. That is what this protocol does — it rebuilds the barrier itself, then everything else you do for your health finally has a foundation to work on.

The core idea

Repair the gut lining → quiet the inflammatory traffic to the brain → mood, focus, energy, digestion, and long-term brain health all improve from the same single intervention. You can't out-supplement a damaged supply line.

The 7-day fast start

The powder repairs the lining. These seven days stop you from re-damaging it while it heals. Check each one off.

DAY 1
Start Biome NS Ultra with your first meal. Drink water through the day — repair is hydration-dependent.
DAY 2
Purge the seed oils. Canola, soybean, sunflower, safflower, corn oil — check your pantry and your takeout habits. These are the most consistent everyday insult to the gut lining.
DAY 3
Protein-first breakfast — open the day with 30–40 g of lean protein (eggs, meat, fish, Greek yogurt). Protein carries the raw material the lining rebuilds with.
DAY 4
Give eating a start and an end. Try a 9 a.m.–6 p.m. eating window today. No food within 3 hours of bed — late eating is a double hit on gut repair and deep sleep.
DAY 5
Walk 30 minutes after your largest meal. Post-meal movement improves glucose handling and motility — the gentlest gut therapy there is.
DAY 6
Alcohol-free day. Alcohol directly erodes the barrier you're paying to rebuild. Notice how you sleep tonight versus a drinking night.
DAY 7
Real-food day. Single-ingredient foods only — meat, fish, eggs, vegetables, fruit, rice. If it has a mascot or a health claim on the box, it waits.
The one rule that carries the week

Take the powder every day, and remove one insult at a time. You don't have to be perfect on all seven — you have to be consistent on the powder and directionally better on the rest. Repair rewards the man or woman who simply keeps showing up.
