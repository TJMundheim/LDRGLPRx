## 3. Affiliate Platform Primer

Most companies outsource affiliate tracking to one of four major networks. You need an account on each:

### Impact (impact.com)
The dominant platform for premium consumer wellness brands. HigherDose, BonCharge, Waterdrop, Plunge, Thrive Market, Crowd Cow, ButcherBox, WHOOP, and Eight Sleep all use Impact. Create one Impact account at impact.com and apply to each brand as a publisher from within the platform. Impact tracks clicks, commissions, and payments in one dashboard.

### ShareASale (shareasale.com)
Owned by Awin. Used by San Francisco Salt Co., Berkey, and Primal Kitchen. Create a free publisher account at shareasale.com, then search the merchant directory for each brand and submit an application.

### CJ Affiliate (cj.com)
Previously Commission Junction. Used by Vital Choice, Lumosity, and Garmin. Same model: one publisher account, apply to each merchant individually.

### Amazon Associates (affiliate-program.amazon.com)
The catch-all fallback. Any product sold on Amazon can be linked via your Associates account for a commission (typically 3–8% depending on category). Useful for brands that don't run their own program but sell heavily on Amazon: TriField TF2, Cornet, Life-flo, Sherpa Pink, Nicorette, Carex, Verilux, Temtop.

### Direct Programs
Many wellness brands — especially in the sauna, red light, and cold plunge categories — run their own in-house affiliate programs (often powered by Referral Hero, Recharge, or a custom portal). These are listed as "Direct" in the platform column. Apply on the brand's own website.

## 4. Recommended Workflow

**Step 1 — Create platform accounts first.**
Before applying to any individual brand, set up accounts on Impact, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, and Amazon Associates. This takes about 60 minutes total and unlocks access to ~40% of the brands on this list immediately.

**Step 2 — Work the High priority rows first.**
High-priority brands are listed at the top of the spreadsheet. These are the ones with the highest revenue potential per referral (high AOV, strong commission rates, or recurring subscription models): HigherDose, BonCharge, Joovv, SaunaSpace, Clearlight, Plunge, Ice Barrel, ButcherBox, Ancestral Supplements, Thrive Market, Function Health, WHOOP, Oura Ring, Eight Sleep, BrainHQ/Posit Science, Sunnyside, Reframe, Genova Diagnostics, Diagnostic Solutions.

**Step 3 — Apply with practitioner context.**
In every affiliate application where there's a free-text field, identify yourself as a functional medicine physician running a structured wellness program (the 4M Program) for men 35–60. This increases approval rates and often unlocks practitioner-tier commission rates that are higher than the standard influencer rate.

**Step 4 — Request a dedicated affiliate manager.**
After approval, respond to the welcome email and ask: *"Is there a dedicated affiliate manager I can work with to optimize our campaign?"* Most platforms at the Impact/ShareASale/CJ level assign managers once you've generated initial traffic. Request this proactively — don't wait.

**Step 5 — Ask for a rate uplift after 30 days.**
Once you have 30 days of traffic data (even modest numbers), email your affiliate manager and ask for a commission rate review. Practitioners who can demonstrate consistent, high-quality referral traffic almost always get rate bumps. Standard rates are a floor, not a ceiling.

**Step 6 — For "Direct Outreach Required" brands**, use the phone number or contact form listed. Lead with: *"I'm a physician running a structured men's wellness program and I recommend [product] to my clients. I'd like to discuss a formal referral or affiliate arrangement."* Small brands (Aero-Tech, Cold Stoic, Penguin Chillers, etc.) are often willing to negotiate custom referral deals that are better than any standard affiliate program.

## 7. Honest Caveat About Contact Information

**Affiliate manager direct contact info is gated for most companies.** This is by design — companies protect their affiliate managers from cold outreach until they've screened applicants. Of the ~100+ rows in this spreadsheet:

- **~15 rows** have a published affiliate contact email (Earthing.com, Joovv, SaunaSpace, Ice Barrel, ButcherBox, BrainHQ/Posit Science, Inside Tracker, and a handful of others)
- **~40 rows** are handled entirely through a platform (Impact, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, Amazon Associates) — no direct email needed, just apply in-platform
- **~30 rows** require direct outreach via a contact form or phone call — especially the smaller, niche brands (cold plunge chillers, incandescent bulb manufacturers, EMF shielding suppliers, commercial ice machine brands)
- **~15 rows** are marked Not Applicable (free resources, direct TJ partnerships, commercial equipment dealer models)

**The phone numbers and emails in this spreadsheet are public-facing main company contacts** — not direct affiliate manager lines. The path to a named manager is: apply → get approved → respond to welcome email and ask for a manager. That is the industry-standard path, and it works.

Do not pay any third party to "get you direct affiliate manager contacts." Those lists are scraped, outdated, and will not convert. The application form is the correct first step.
