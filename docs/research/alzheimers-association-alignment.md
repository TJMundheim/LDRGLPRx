## 1. Organization Snapshot

The **Alzheimer's Association** is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit, **founded April 10, 1980**, headquartered in Chicago, with a Public Policy Office in Washington D.C. and **74 chapters across the United States**.

**Scale (FY 2024):**
- Annual revenue: **~$458M**
- Employees: **~2,514**
- Mission statement: *"Lead the way to end Alzheimer's and all other dementia — by accelerating global research, driving risk reduction and early detection, and maximizing quality care and support."*

**Flagship campaign — Walk to End Alzheimer's:**
- Held annually in **600+ communities**
- 2024: **350,000+ participants, $100M+ raised**
- National presenting sponsor: **Edward Jones** (10 consecutive years)
- 2025 national sponsors: **Axsome, Biogen, Eisai, Salesforce**
- Note: Biogen and Eisai are makers of Leqembi (lecanemab), the FDA-approved Alzheimer's drug — telling about who they accept as partners.

**Distinction from Alzheimer's Foundation of America (AFA):** Different organization. Smaller (~$10M revenue vs $458M), more support-services focused. The Alzheimer's Association is the dominant national org and the one with statistical/research authority.

**Other key initiatives:**
- **2026 Facts and Figures Report** — annual flagship publication, widely cited in journalism
- **Brain Health At Work™** — free workplace wellness program (relevant to our positioning — see endorsement section)
- **The Longest Day** — June 21 awareness/fundraising event
- **Ride to End ALZ** — cycling fundraiser
- **AAIC** — Alzheimer's Association International Conference (research)

## 2. 2026 Facts and Figures — Statistics For Our Copy

These are the most-citable statistics from the 2026 report, ranked by relevance to My4MLife's positioning:

### A) The awareness-action gap (use these everywhere)

> **"99% of Americans value brain health equally to physical health, yet only 9% report knowing 'a lot' about maintaining it."**

This is the single most powerful sentence in the entire report for our copy. It exactly justifies the existence of My4MLife. Consider deploying on:
- Homepage hero subhead or below-fold callout
- About page mission section
- Membership page top
- Founder essay introduction

> **"75% of Americans believe lifestyle habits support brain health, but only 46% strongly link these behaviors to reducing dementia risk."**

This is the conviction gap — people *suspect* it matters but don't *believe* it strongly enough to act. 4M's job is to close that gap.

> **"2 in 3 Americans want brain health guidance from providers; only 14% have discussed it with their doctor."**

This is **literally the My4MLife wedge.** 53-percentage-point gap between desire and provision. Our consult fills that gap. Use this on the `/consult` page.

### B) Scale of the problem (use these to establish stakes)

> **More than 7 million Americans currently live with Alzheimer's.**

> **By 2050, that number is projected to reach nearly 13 million.**

> **Lifetime risk at age 45: 1 in 5 for women, 1 in 10 for men.**

(For a male-focused brand this 1-in-10 stat is most cite-worthy. Ten percent. Of the men we're talking to. Will get this. Without intervention.)

### C) Mortality and burden

> **1 in 3 older adults dies with Alzheimer's or another dementia — more than breast cancer and prostate cancer combined.**

> **Unpaid caregiver care valued at $446.3 billion in 2025 — more than 17 times McDonald's 2024 revenue.**

These two are useful for "what's at stake" framing — particularly for the `/consult` and `/membership` pages where we're asking the user to act.

### D) Citation format

The Association requires only standard attribution for use of their public statistics. Cite as:
*"Alzheimer's Association. 2026 Alzheimer's Disease Facts and Figures. https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/facts-figures"*

No permission needed. This is **free positioning ammunition we should be using already.**

## 3. Prevention Alignment — 10 Healthy Habits vs. 4M Framework

The Alzheimer's Association's official prevention guidance is **"10 Healthy Habits for Your Brain."** Side-by-side mapping:

| # | AA Recommendation | 4M Pillar / My4MLife Page | Alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Challenge your mind** — learn new skills, artistic activities | Mind pillar / `/pillars/mind` / Membership Insider tier educational content | **Strong** |
| 2 | **Stay in school** — formal education, continuing education | Mind pillar / `/solutions/health-knowledge` (blog + app education content) | **Strong** |
| 3 | **Get moving** — exercise, raise heart rate, blood flow to brain | Muscle pillar / `/pillars/muscle` / GLP-1 + body composition protocols | **Strong** |
| 4 | **Protect your head** — helmets, seatbelts, prevent injury | Mitigate pillar / `/solutions/pain-acute` / acute injury protocol | **Partial** (we cover acute injury; head-injury prevention not explicitly featured) |
| 5 | **Be smoke-free** — quit smoking | Mitigate pillar / `/solutions/substance-use` | **Strong** |
| 6 | **Control your blood pressure** | Mitigate pillar / consult lab panel includes cardiovascular markers | **Strong** |
| 7 | **Manage diabetes** — diet, activity, medication | Mitigate / Muscle / GLP-1 path on `/solutions/weight` + nutrition + metabolic labs | **Strong** |
| 8 | **Eat right** — vegetables, lean protein, less processed | Mitigate pillar / `/solutions/nutrition` (ancestral sourcing standard) | **Strong** |
| 9 | **Maintain a healthy weight** | Muscle / `/solutions/weight` (GLP-1 + body comp) | **Strong** |
| 10 | **Sleep well** — quality sleep, address disorders | Mitigate / `/solutions/sleep` | **Strong** |

**Result: 9/10 strong alignment, 1/10 partial.**

### What this means for positioning

We can credibly say:
- *"My4MLife's 4M framework independently aligns with 9 of 10 Alzheimer's Association prevention recommendations."*
- *"The Alzheimer's Association recommends 10 healthy habits for your brain. The 4M framework operationalizes them — and adds the clinical infrastructure to actually execute."*

We should **not** say:
- *"Endorsed by..."* / *"In partnership with..."* — these would be misleading absent a formal relationship.
- *"Approved by..."* / *"Recommended by..."* — same.

### Quick fix to close the 1 partial gap

Add brief head-injury / concussion content to `/solutions/pain-acute` or `/pillars/mitigate`. One paragraph. We already cover acute injury triage; we can extend to "concussion as a leading driver of long-term cognitive decline — protect your head." Then it's 10/10.

## 4. Endorsement Odds — Honest Assessment

### The hard truth

**Formal endorsement is essentially impossible.** The Alzheimer's Association does not endorse commercial third-party health platforms. They have not done so for any direct competitor (Hims, Ro, Function Health, Bryan Johnson's Blueprint, none of them carry an AA endorsement). Their corporate partnerships are with:
- **Pharma giants** that make Alzheimer's-specific drugs (Biogen — Aduhelm; Eisai — Leqembi)
- **Financial services** (Edward Jones — alignment with caregiving/inheritance financial planning)
- **Tech infrastructure** (Salesforce — they use the platform)
- **Insurers and healthcare systems** at the chapter level

A small telehealth + lifestyle platform isn't on this list and won't be — not because we're wrong, but because they categorically don't.

### What IS achievable, in order of likelihood

**Tier A (high probability, low value, free) — Statistical citation**

Cite their public statistics with proper attribution on our marketing site. No permission needed. We should be doing this **today.** Specifically:
- The 99% / 9% awareness-action gap on the homepage
- The "2 in 3 want it from doctors / 14% have gotten it" stat on the consult page
- The 1-in-10 lifetime-risk-for-men stat on the cognitive solution page

**What it requires:** literally nothing. Just deploy the copy. Estimated value: high, because it instantly positions us as data-aware and authoritative without overclaiming relationship.

**Tier B (medium probability, real visibility) — Walk to End Alzheimer's chapter sponsorship**

Sponsor a local chapter Walk to End Alzheimer's event. Sponsorship tiers exist at the chapter level (typical range $2,500–$25,000 for local visibility, $50,000+ for regional, $100,000+ for national-tier). My4MLife branding appears in event materials, at the event, in chapter newsletters.

**What it requires:** budget (ideally Q4 2026 or Q1 2027 once we have revenue) + a local chapter relationship. Texas or wherever TJ's home chapter is.

**Estimated value:** medium-high. Real-world brand presence to a perfectly-targeted demographic (caregivers + at-risk adults). Also unlocks soft AA chapter-level relationships for Tier C.

**Tier C (medium probability if Tier B done first) — Speaking / educational content**

Dr. TJ presents at a local AA chapter educational event as a clinician (Doctor of Chiropractic with 30+ years of practice, brain-health framework). Many chapters host quarterly educational events for members and the public. A clinician with a coherent brain-health thesis is exactly what they're looking for.

**What it requires:** existing chapter relationship (Tier B helps), a presentation deck on the 4M framework framed as patient education (not platform marketing), being explicit that you are NOT representing the Association.

**Estimated value:** high — Dr. TJ becomes a known clinician within a chapter community. This is how reputational endorsement actually accumulates over time.

**Tier D (low probability, longer arc) — Brain Health At Work co-branding**

Brain Health At Work™ is the AA's free workplace wellness program. It's possible (not guaranteed) that an aligned platform like My4MLife could become a referral partner — companies running BHAW could direct interested employees to My4MLife as a deeper protocol option.

**What it requires:** the AA seeing My4MLife as legitimate and complementary, not competitive. This means **published outcome data on a real cohort** + **explicit positioning as protocol-and-coaching, not as an Alzheimer's treatment**. We are not treating Alzheimer's. We are operationalizing the upstream prevention guidance the AA itself publishes.

**Estimated value:** very high if landed. Realistic timeline: 18–36 months out, after we have a member base and outcome data.

**Tier E (essentially zero probability) — Formal endorsement / co-branded campaign**

Don't pursue. They don't do this. Continued effort here is wasted energy.

### Recommended sequence

1. **This week / next week:** Deploy the citation copy on homepage, consult, cognitive solution. Free, immediate, high-impact.
2. **At launch + 90 days:** Reach out to TJ's local Alzheimer's Association chapter (find via [https://www.alz.org/local](https://www.alz.org/local)). Introduce the platform as a brain-health-first protocol; ask about sponsorship opportunities for the next Walk.
3. **At launch + 6 months:** Sponsor at a comfortable tier ($2,500–$5,000 chapter-level). Establish presence.
4. **At launch + 12 months:** Pitch Dr. TJ as a chapter educational speaker. By this point we have outcome data and a chapter relationship.
5. **At launch + 18+ months:** Evaluate Brain Health At Work co-branding once we have published outcomes.
6. **Never:** Pursue formal endorsement. Wasted runway.

### What would make AA take us seriously

If the goal is to maximize legitimacy in their eyes, prioritize building these in order:
- **Real clinical outcome data** on a member cohort (cognitive scores pre/post protocol)
- **A peer-reviewed publication or preprint** on the 4M framework, even a small one
- **Dr. TJ's clinician credentials front-and-center** (already done — DC, 30 years, NBCE-certified since 1994)
- **Compliance posture** that's clearly above-board (already in good shape — telemedicine partner, no direct medical claims)
- **A clinical advisory board** with at least one MD or PhD-level researcher (this is the missing piece — would unlock conversations the platform can't currently have)

A clinical advisory board is the single highest-leverage credential gap. Adding even one named MD or neuropsychology PhD to the platform changes the conversation with every gatekeeper organization, not just the AA.
