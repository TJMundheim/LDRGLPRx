# MitoVita Sexual Performance — Evidence Compendium

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Purpose:** Document the published evidence for each MitoVita ingredient in the sexual-performance / erectile-function domain. Honest grading — strong vs moderate vs indirect — so marketing copy stays defensible under hyper-analytical scrutiny.

---

## Headline finding

**MitoVita's vascular ingredients (L-citrulline + beetroot extract) have the strongest OTC published evidence in the sexual performance space.** Creatine's direct evidence for sexual function is thin — the published literature supports its role in cognitive function and lean-mass preservation, with sexual benefits being indirect (via energy, mood, body composition, downstream cardiovascular health).

**Marketing implication:** Lead the sexual-performance positioning with the L-citrulline + beetroot NO story. Position creatine as the cognitive + muscle + visceral-fat layer that pairs with the NO mechanism. Don't overclaim creatine for ED specifically — it's a credibility weak link by the standards of the no-weak-links rule.

## L-citrulline — strong evidence (THE primary OTC story)

### Landmark RCT — Cormio et al. 2011

- **Citation:** Cormio L, De Siati M, Lorusso F, et al. "Oral L-citrulline supplementation improves erection hardness in men with mild erectile dysfunction." *Urology*. 2011 Jan;77(1):119-22. [PubMed 21195829](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21195829/)
- **Design:** Single-blind, placebo-controlled. 24 men, mean age 56.5. One month placebo run-in → one month L-citrulline 1.5g/day
- **Primary result:** Erection Hardness Score improved from 3 (mild ED) to 4 (normal):
  - **L-citrulline arm: 50% of men improved (P < .01)**
  - Placebo arm: 8.3% improved
- **Secondary result:** Monthly intercourse frequency rose from 1.37 → 2.3 (P < .01)
- **Safety:** No adverse events
- **Author conclusion (verbatim from abstract):** *"L-citrulline supplementation has been proved to be safe and psychologically well accepted. Its effectiveness is lower than that of PDE5 inhibitors, although it has been demonstrated to improve erection hardness in patients with mild ED."*

**Why it matters:** This is the most-cited published RCT for any OTC nutraceutical in the ED domain. The mechanism (citrulline → arginine → NO → vasodilation) is the same upstream pathway PDE5 inhibitors exploit downstream by blocking degradation.

### Supporting mechanism — citrulline vs arginine bioavailability

- L-citrulline is preferred over L-arginine for NO support because it bypasses hepatic first-pass metabolism that limits L-arginine bioavailability
- 1.5g–6g/day L-citrulline produces measurable plasma arginine increases and endothelial function improvements (Bailey et al., Pérez-Guisado et al., multiple replications)

### Long-term L-arginine — Spanish multicenter RCT 2021

- **Citation:** "Long-term high-dose l-arginine supplementation in patients with vasculogenic erectile dysfunction: a multicentre, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial." *Journal of Endocrinological Investigation*. 2021. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40618-021-01704-3)
- Confirmed the L-arginine/citrulline pathway approach for vasculogenic ED in a larger, longer trial design

## Beetroot / dietary nitrate — moderate-to-strong evidence (the second-mechanism ED pathway)

### Mechanism

Dietary nitrate (beetroot) → reduced by oral commensal bacteria to nitrite → reduced to NO via the **nitrate–nitrite–NO pathway**. This is a *parallel* and *additive* mechanism to the citrulline→arginine→NO pathway. Two pathways feeding one outcome (penile arterial vasodilation).

### L-citrulline + Beet Root combination — 2024 JSM study

- **Citation:** "(290) L-Citrulline and Beet Root Supplement Improves Nighttime Erections." *Journal of Sexual Medicine*. 2024 Supplement 7. [Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/jsm/article/21/Supplement_7/qdae167.281/7919081) | [Full text](https://www.longdom.org/open-access/lcitrulline-and-beet-root-supplement-improves-nighttime-erections-1102845.html)
- **Design:** Crossover, 10 men, mean age 52, ingestion 1 hour before sleep
- **Result:** Objective measurement (TechRing wearable) showed improved erection quality vs baseline
- **Significance:** Direct evidence for the *combination* MitoVita uses — citrulline + beetroot — measured objectively, not via self-report

### Foundational nitrate / endothelial function literature

- Bailey SJ, Winyard P, et al. — multiple beetroot juice studies on endothelial function and BP reduction
- Wightman EL et al. 2015 — beetroot juice acutely increased cerebral blood flow during cognitive tasks (relevant to cognitive + ED dual-mechanism story)

### Systematic reviews

- "Herbal dietary supplements for erectile dysfunction: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized-controlled trials." Recent meta. [PMC12902307](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12902307/). Reviewed RCTs in herbal ED supplements; the nitrate / NO-precursor pathway emerges as the most mechanistically supported intervention.

## Pycnogenol® (pine bark extract) — strong evidence (the optional premium layer)

### Stanislavov & Nikolova 2003 and follow-ups

- Pycnogenol + L-arginine combination significantly improved erectile function in men with mild-to-moderate ED
- Multiple replications: Pycnogenol enhances endogenous NO production via eNOS upregulation
- Why it's optional in MitoVita: cost (~$1,200–1,600/kg vs $45–60/kg for citrulline) — adds material per-serving COGS. Recommend reserving for premium MitoVita variant.

## Creatine — strong cognitive + muscle, INDIRECT for sexual function

### What the evidence DOES support

- **Cognitive function** — Rae 2003, Avgerinos 2018 meta, Gordji-Nejad 2024. Strong.
- **Sarcopenia / lean mass** — Chilibeck 2017 meta. Strong.
- **Visceral fat reduction (indirect via lean mass)** — Forbes 2022. Moderate.
- **Mitochondrial ATP buffering in smooth muscle (including corpus cavernosum)** — mechanistically plausible. Phosphocreatine is a known ATP donor in all tissues with phosphocreatine kinase, which includes corpus cavernosum smooth muscle. *But there is no published RCT testing creatine specifically on erectile function as a primary endpoint.*

### What the evidence does NOT support

- A direct claim that "creatine improves erections" or "creatine treats ED"
- No published peer-reviewed RCT exists with creatine and EHS (Erection Hardness Score) or IIEF (International Index of Erectile Function) as the primary outcome
- Contested studies on creatine + DHT (Van der Merwe 2009 South African rugby players showed 56% DHT increase, but this has not been reliably replicated and isn't an ED-outcome study anyway)

### Why we still include creatine in MitoVita for the sexual-performance positioning

1. **Mechanistic plausibility** — corpus cavernosum smooth muscle is energetically demanding under load. Phosphocreatine availability is a reasonable contributor to sustained function under that load. This is a mechanism-grounded inference, not a claim we can support with an RCT.
2. **Indirect benefits via the four-pillar story** — body composition improvement, mood support, sleep architecture support, energy capacity. All of these affect sexual function indirectly.
3. **The cardiovascular link** — Creatine improves training capacity, which improves cardiovascular health, which improves endothelial function, which improves erectile function. Indirect but real.

### How to phrase creatine in sexual-performance copy (compliance-safe)

✅ OK to say:
- "Creatine supports mitochondrial ATP capacity in all energetically demanding smooth muscle, including the corpus cavernosum"
- "Pairs the documented sexual-performance ingredients (L-citrulline + beetroot) with the cellular-energy support that demanding tissues depend on"
- "The lean-mass and cardiovascular benefits of creatine compound the upstream factors that determine erectile function over decades"

❌ NOT OK to say (no published RCT support):
- "Creatine improves erections"
- "Creatine treats ED"
- "Creatine increases erection hardness"

## How to position MitoVita for sexual performance in copy

### The honest, defensible positioning

> *"MitoVita pairs the two most-published OTC mechanisms for sexual performance — L-citrulline (the only OTC nutraceutical with a published RCT showing improved Erection Hardness Score) and dietary nitrate from beetroot (the parallel NO pathway) — with creatine, which supports the mitochondrial ATP capacity of all the tissues that perform under load: brain, muscle, and the corpus cavernosum included."*

### Top three citations to embed on the ED page

1. **Cormio et al. 2011** — L-citrulline 1.5g/day, 50% of men with mild ED improved EHS from 3 to 4 (P<.01)
2. **2024 JSM crossover** — L-citrulline + beetroot combination, objective wearable measurement showed improved nighttime erection quality
3. **Mechanism statement** — The same nitric oxide pathway PDE5 inhibitors exploit (downstream blockade of NO degradation), MitoVita supports upstream (NO production via two parallel pathways)

### What we should NOT do

- Quote the Cormio 2011 50% number without "in men with mild ED" context (overclaim)
- Imply MitoVita is equivalent to or substitutes for PDE5 inhibitors (FDA drug-claim territory)
- Make creatine-specific sexual-performance claims (no RCT support — no-weak-links rule applies)
