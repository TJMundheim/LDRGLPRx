# Founder Photo Shoot List — Dr. TJ

Purpose: real founder photography to replace/augment Midjourney scenes and unlock the
**founder-hero vs. boardroom-hero PostHog A/B** on the homepage. One half-day shoot covers
everything below. Book a local photographer who shoots executive editorial (not "corporate
headshot mill") — show them this doc.

## Brand grade (give this to the photographer)

- Palette: deep navy + warm gold accents; warm, low-key lighting; dawn/dusk golden hour where possible
- Mood: peak-power executive, calm authority — never "active grandpa," never lab-coat stock
- Wardrobe: navy blazer or quality knit, open collar, no tie; one outfit change max
- NO: alcohol anywhere in frame (green/iced tea only), visible brand logos, whitecoat-with-stethoscope clichés
- Deliverables: full-res + 1800px-wide web crops, landscape 3:2 for heroes, a few 4:5 verticals for blog/social

## Shot list (priority order)

### A. Hero candidates (the A/B test — get these right)
1. **Boardroom dawn, founder edition** — TJ at the head of a conference table, city or window light behind, reviewing a single page. Matches the current homepage hero's energy so the A/B isolates "real founder" as the variable. Landscape, generous negative space on the left for headline copy.
2. **Book-cover echo** — same styling as the *Begin with the End in Mind* cover portrait, but wider crop with environment. Ties the site to the book on sight.
3. **Direct-to-camera authority portrait** — chest-up, navy background or dark environment, slight warmth. This is the "credibility" shot TJ asked for ("a much larger picture of me like the one on the cover of the book").

### B. Founder-at-work (About page, consult pages, press)
4. Reviewing labs/charts on a tablet at a desk — the async-telemedicine visual (no patient in frame, no PHI on screen — use a blank or mock chart).
5. Writing in a leather journal, fountain pen — pairs with the cognitive/mind imagery already live.
6. On a video call, warm home-office light — the weekly-Zoom / cohort-leader visual.

### C. Protocol-in-life (solution pages, guide, social proof)
7. Morning walk, golden hour, athletic-casual — fasted-training visual.
8. Kitchen: plating a ribeye / eggs, cast iron, green tea in frame — protein-first + "Keto Cattle Baron" energy.
9. Pouring green tea at a dinner table set for guests — the no-alcohol social scene, done aspirationally.
10. Strength: doorway pull-up bar or heel-elevated squat, controlled and dignified (form over sweat).

### D. Legacy / accountability-target (emotional layer)
11. With family if available — grandkids, boat, outdoors; faces of family can be averted if preferred. This is the "name a specific face" accountability visual.
12. Contemplative: looking out a window or across water at dusk, back three-quarter angle. Pairs with "Begin with the end in mind."

## Coverage math
40% leadership (A + B) / 25% active lifestyle (C7, C10) / 15% family (D11) / 10% contemplative (D12) / 10% protocol (C8, C9) — per the locked photography direction.

## After the shoot
1. Fable reviews selects (hands, logos, grade) and optimizes to ~1800px JPEG q80.
2. Place hero candidate → launch PostHog A/B: founder-hero vs. current boardroom hero on `/`.
3. Distribute B/C/D shots per `docs/design/imagery-map.md`; retire the Midjourney founder-adjacent scenes they replace.
