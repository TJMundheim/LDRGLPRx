My4MLife · App restructure · 2026-07-06

One week, one Move, twelve times.

Three structures for the same idea: the dashboard shows results only (MindSpan Score, risk, trends) and the week is where work gets logged — a standing daily tape plus one Move everyone takes that week, tracked straight into the score. Same Mission Control skin throughout; what changes is what the app opens on and how the week is shaped. Pick one, mark it up, and we build.

1

The app opens on today's tape. Score rides on top as a live chip — tap it for the full dashboard. Fastest possible daily loop; the dashboard is the reward you visit, not the door you walk through.

The 12-week Move ladder — draft

Every week has the identical tape. The only thing that changes is the Move — one action, same for every man in the cohort, tracked into the MindSpan Score. Months carry the locked 4M arc. Week 12 closes the loop: retake the assessment, watch the score move against baseline. All twelve are placeholders for you to edit.

Month 1 · MITIGATE

stop the insults

W1Start Biome NS Ultra — seal the gut, every morning
W2Hold the 9–6 window all seven days
W3Protein-first 30–40g at every fast-break
W4Pantry purge — zero seed oils in the house

Month 2 · MUSCLE

build the engine

W5Strength ×2, log the lifts
W6Fasted dawn walk ×5
W7Add one intensity day — hills or sprints
W8Sleep week: 3-hr cutoff + cold dark room

Month 3 · MOTIVATE → MIND

lock it in for life

W9Name your face — write who you're doing this for
W10Heat or cold exposure ×2
W11Book your labs — measure, don't guess
W12Retake the assessment — new MindSpan baseline

How to choose

Pick 1 if the daily habit is sacred and everything else serves it. Pick 2 if the MindSpan Score is the product and every open should sell it. Pick 3 if the week — Zoom to Zoom, cohort together on one Move — is the truest shape of the program.

They share one skeleton underneath (same data, same tape, same ladder), so your tweaks can mix parts — e.g. Direction 3's week card with Direction 2's dashboard as the Sunday view. Mark it up and we build.
